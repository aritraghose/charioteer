3.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन । / तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥
LIT: If buddhi is thought by you superior to action, O Janārdana, then why do you engage me in terrible action, O Keśava?
WORDS: jyāyasī = superior; cet = if; karmaṇaḥ = to action; te = by you; matā = thought; buddhiḥ = buddhi; janārdana = O Janārdana; tat = then; kim = why; karmaṇi = in action; ghore = terrible; mām = me; niyojayasi = you engage; keśava = O Keśava

3.2 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे । / तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥
LIT: By a statement that is as if mixed, you as if bewilder my buddhi. Having decided, tell that one thing by which I may reach the better.
WORDS: vyāmiśreṇa = mixed; iva = as if; vākyena = by a statement; buddhim = buddhi; mohayasi = you bewilder; iva = as if; me = my; tat = that; ekam = one thing; vada = tell; niścitya = having decided; yena = by which; śreyaḥ = the better; aham = I; āpnuyām = may reach

3.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: लोकेऽस्मिन् द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ । / ज्ञानयोगेन साङ्ख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥
LIT: In this world a firm standing of two kinds was formerly declared by me, O sinless one: that of the followers of sāṅkhya by the yoga of knowledge, that of the yogīs by the yoga of action.
WORDS: loke = in the world; asmin = in this; dvi-vidhā = of two kinds; niṣṭhā = firm standing; purā = formerly; proktā = declared; mayā = by me; anagha = O sinless one; jñāna-yogena = by the yoga of knowledge; sāṅkhyānām = of the followers of sāṅkhya; karma-yogena = by the yoga of action; yoginām = of the yogīs
NOTE: "Formerly" can mean long ago, at the beginning of things, or it can mean earlier in this same conversation.

3.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते । / न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥
LIT: A person does not reach actionlessness from not beginning actions, and he does not come to perfection from renouncing alone.
WORDS: na = not; karmaṇām = of actions; anārambhāt = from not beginning; naiṣkarmyam = actionlessness; puruṣaḥ = a person; aśnute = reaches; na = not; ca = and; saṁnyasanāt = from renouncing; eva = alone; siddhim = perfection; samadhigacchati = he comes to

3.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् । / कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥
LIT: For no one ever stays even for a moment not doing action. For everyone, without choice, is made to do action by the guṇas born of nature.

3.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् । / इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥
LIT: Whoever sits, having restrained the organs of action, remembering with the mind the objects of the senses, he, one whose self is deluded, is called one of false conduct.

3.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन । / कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥
LIT: But whoever, having controlled the senses by the mind, O Arjuna, begins the yoga of action with the organs of action, unattached, he excels.

3.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः । / शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः ॥
LIT: You, do prescribed action, for action is superior to non-action. And even the maintenance of your body would not succeed from non-action.
WORDS: niyatam = prescribed; kuru = do; karma = action; tvam = you; karma = action; jyāyaḥ = superior; hi = for; akarmaṇaḥ = to non-action; śarīra-yātrā = the maintenance of the body; api = even; ca = and; te = your; na = not; prasiddhyet = would succeed; akarmaṇaḥ = from non-action
NOTE: "Prescribed action" can also be read as "do action constantly", taking the word to describe how one acts and not which action.

3.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः । / तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥
LIT: Apart from action for the purpose of sacrifice, this world is bound by action. Perform action for that purpose, O son of Kuntī, freed from attachment.
WORDS: yajña-arthāt = for the purpose of sacrifice; karmaṇaḥ = from action; anyatra = apart; lokaḥ = world; ayam = this; karma-bandhanaḥ = bound by action; tad-artham = for that purpose; karma = action; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; mukta-saṅgaḥ = freed from attachment; samācara = perform

3.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः । / अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक् ॥
LIT: Having created creatures together with sacrifice, the lord of creatures formerly said: by this you shall bring forth. Let this be yours, yielding like milk the desires wished for.
WORDS: saha-yajñāḥ = together with sacrifice; prajāḥ = creatures; sṛṣṭvā = having created; purā = formerly; uvāca = said; prajāpatiḥ = the lord of creatures; anena = by this; prasaviṣyadhvam = you shall bring forth; eṣaḥ = this; vaḥ = your; astu = let it be; iṣṭa-kāma-dhuk = yielding like milk the desires wished for

3.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः । / परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ ॥
LIT: Nourish the gods by this. Let those gods nourish you. Nourishing one another, you will attain the highest good.
WORDS: devān = the gods; bhāvayata = nourish; anena = by this; te = those; devāḥ = gods; bhāvayantu = let them nourish; vaḥ = you; parasparam = one another; bhāvayantaḥ = nourishing; śreyaḥ = good; param = the highest; avāpsyatha = you will attain

3.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः । / तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः ॥
LIT: For the gods, nourished by sacrifice, will give to you the enjoyments wished for. Whoever enjoys the things given by them without giving to these, he is only a thief.
WORDS: iṣṭān = wished for; bhogān = enjoyments; hi = for; vaḥ = to you; devāḥ = the gods; dāsyante = will give; yajña-bhāvitāḥ = nourished by sacrifice; taiḥ = by them; dattān = the things given; apradāya = without giving; ebhyaḥ = to these; yaḥ = who; bhuṅkte = enjoys; stenaḥ = a thief; eva = only; saḥ = he

3.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषैः । / भुञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् ॥
LIT: The good, eating what is left of the sacrifice, are freed from all faults. But the wicked who cook for the sake of the self, they eat sin.
WORDS: yajña-śiṣṭa-āśinaḥ = eating what is left of the sacrifice; santaḥ = the good; mucyante = are freed; sarva-kilbiṣaiḥ = from all faults; bhuñjate = eat; te = they; tu = but; agham = sin; pāpāḥ = the wicked; ye = who; pacanti = cook; ātma-kāraṇāt = for the sake of the self
NOTE: "For the sake of the self" here most naturally means "only for themselves", with no deeper sense of the self intended.

3.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः । / यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः ॥
LIT: From food beings come to be. From rain is the arising of food. From sacrifice rain comes to be. Sacrifice is arising from action.
WORDS: annāt = from food; bhavanti = come to be; bhūtāni = beings; parjanyāt = from rain; anna-sambhavaḥ = the arising of food; yajñāt = from sacrifice; bhavati = comes to be; parjanyaḥ = rain; yajñaḥ = sacrifice; karma-samudbhavaḥ = arising from action

3.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् । / तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥
LIT: Know action as arising from brahman, and brahman as arising from the imperishable. Therefore the all-pervading brahman is always established in sacrifice.
WORDS: karma = action; brahma-udbhavam = arising from brahman; viddhi = know; brahma = brahman; akṣara-samudbhavam = arising from the imperishable; tasmāt = therefore; sarva-gatam = all-pervading; brahma = brahman; nityam = always; yajñe = in sacrifice; pratiṣṭhitam = established
NOTE: "Arising from brahman" is often read with brahman meaning the Vedas, so that action arises from the Vedas and the Vedas from the imperishable. Others read brahman here as nature.

3.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः । / अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थ स जीवति ॥
LIT: Whoever here does not keep turning the wheel thus set turning, whose life is sin, whose delight is the senses, he lives in vain, O son of Pṛthā.
WORDS: evam = thus; pravartitam = set turning; cakram = the wheel; na = not; anuvartayati = keeps turning; iha = here; yaḥ = who; agha-āyuḥ = whose life is sin; indriya-ārāmaḥ = whose delight is the senses; mogham = in vain; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; saḥ = he; jīvati = lives

3.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च मानवः । / आत्मन्येव च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते ॥
LIT: But the human being who would be one whose delight is in the self alone, and satisfied by the self, and content in the self alone, for him something to be done does not exist.
WORDS: yaḥ = who; tu = but; ātma-ratiḥ = whose delight is in the self; eva = alone; syāt = would be; ātma-tṛptaḥ = satisfied by the self; ca = and; mānavaḥ = the human being; ātmani = in the self; eva = alone; ca = and; santuṣṭaḥ = content; tasya = for him; kāryam = something to be done; na = not; vidyate = exists

3.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन । / न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्चिदर्थव्यपाश्रयः ॥
LIT: For him there is indeed not any purpose here by what is done, nor by what is not done. And for him there is not any dependence for a purpose among all beings.
WORDS: na = not; eva = indeed; tasya = for him; kṛtena = by what is done; arthaḥ = a purpose; na = not; akṛtena = by what is not done; iha = here; kaścana = any; na = not; ca = and; asya = for him; sarva-bhūteṣu = among all beings; kaścit = any; artha-vyapāśrayaḥ = dependence for a purpose

3.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तस्मादसक्तः सततं कार्यं कर्म समाचर । / असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पूरुषः ॥
LIT: Therefore, unattached, always perform the action to be done. For performing action unattached, a person reaches the highest.
WORDS: tasmāt = therefore; asaktaḥ = unattached; satatam = always; kāryam = to be done; karma = action; samācara = perform; asaktaḥ = unattached; hi = for; ācaran = performing; karma = action; param = the highest; āpnoti = reaches; pūruṣaḥ = a person

3.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः । / लोकसङ्ग्रहमेवापि सम्पश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि ॥
LIT: For by action alone Janaka and the others attained perfection. Even looking only to the holding together of the world, you ought to act.
WORDS: karmaṇā = by action; eva = alone; hi = for; saṁsiddhim = perfection; āsthitāḥ = attained; janaka-ādayaḥ = Janaka and the others; loka-saṅgraham = the holding together of the world; eva = only; api = even; sampaśyan = looking to; kartum = to act; arhasi = you ought
NOTE: "The holding together of the world" can also be read as "the gathering of the people", that is, keeping people on the right path and not the world in order.

3.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः । / स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते ॥
LIT: Whatever the best one does, that very thing the other person does. What standard he makes, that the world follows.
WORDS: yat = whatever; yat = whatever; ācarati = does; śreṣṭhaḥ = the best one; tat = that; tat = that; eva = very; itaraḥ = other; janaḥ = the person; saḥ = he; yat = what; pramāṇam = standard; kurute = makes; lokaḥ = the world; tat = that; anuvartate = follows

3.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किञ्चन । / नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि ॥

3.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेयं जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः । / मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥

NOTE: Verses 23 to 24 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.
NOTE: "Men follow my path in every way" can also be read as "men would follow my path in every way", making this verse a complete sentence of its own.

3.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम् । / सङ्करस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः ॥

3.25 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत । / कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्चिकीर्षुर्लोकसङ्ग्रहम् ॥

3.26 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् । / जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन् ॥

3.27 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रकृतेः क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वशः । / अहङ्कारविमूढात्मा कर्ताहमिति मन्यते ॥

3.28 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तत्त्ववित्तु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः । / गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्त इति मत्वा न सज्जते ॥

NOTE: "Of the two divisions, of guṇa and of action" can also be read as "of how the self is separate from the guṇas and from action".

3.29 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु । / तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत् ॥

WORDS: prakṛteḥ = of nature; guṇa-sammūḍhāḥ = those deluded by the guṇas; sajjante = they are attached; guṇa-karmasu = in the actions of the guṇas; tān = those; akṛtsna-vidaḥ = who do not know the whole; mandān = slow ones; kṛtsna-vit = the knower of the whole; na = not; vicālayet = should unsettle

3.30 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा । / निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः ॥
LIT: Having laid down all actions in me, with the mind on the self, having become without wishes and without the sense of mine, fight, with fever gone.

3.31 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.32 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.33 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.34 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.35 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.37 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.38 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.39 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

3.43 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
