11.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् । / यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम ॥
LIT: The word which was spoken by you for favour to me, the highest secret, called "concerning the self": by that, this delusion of mine has gone away.
WORDS: mat-anugrahāya = for favour to me; paramam = highest; guhyam = secret; adhyātma-saṁjñitam = called "concerning the self"; yat = which; tvayā = by you; uktam = spoken; vacaḥ = word; tena = by that; mohaḥ = delusion; ayam = this; vigataḥ = gone away; mama = of me

11.2 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया । / त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम् ॥
LIT: For the arising and the passing away of beings have been heard by me in detail from you, O one with eyes like lotus petals, and also the imperishable greatness.
WORDS: bhava-apyayau = the arising and the passing away; hi = for; bhūtānām = of beings; śrutau = heard; vistaraśaḥ = in detail; mayā = by me; tvattaḥ = from you; kamala-patra-akṣa = O one with eyes like lotus petals; māhātmyam = greatness; api = also; ca = and; avyayam = imperishable

11.3 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वमात्मानं परमेश्वर । / द्रष्टुमिच्छामि ते रूपमैश्वरं पुरुषोत्तम ॥
LIT: This is so, as you have said your self to be, O highest lord. I wish to see your lordly form, O highest of persons.
WORDS: evam = so; etat = this; yathā = as; āttha = you have said; tvam = you; ātmānam = your self; parama-īśvara = O highest lord; draṣṭum = to see; icchāmi = I wish; te = of you; rūpam = form; aiśvaram = lordly; puruṣa-uttama = O highest of persons

11.4 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: मन्यसे यदि तच्छक्यं मया द्रष्टुमिति प्रभो । / योगेश्वर ततो मे त्वं दर्शयात्मानमव्ययम् ॥
LIT: If you think thus, that it is able to be seen by me, O lord, then, O lord of yoga, show to me your imperishable self.
WORDS: manyase = you think; yadi = if; tat = that; śakyam = able; mayā = by me; draṣṭum = to be seen; iti = thus; prabho = O lord; yoga-īśvara = O lord of yoga; tataḥ = then; me = to me; tvam = you; darśaya = show; ātmānam = your self; avyayam = imperishable

11.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः । / नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च ॥
LIT: See my forms, O son of Pṛthā, by hundreds and then by thousands, of many kinds, divine, and of many colours and shapes.
WORDS: paśya = see; me = of me; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; rūpāṇi = forms; śataśaḥ = by hundreds; atha = and then; sahasraśaḥ = by thousands; nānā-vidhāni = of many kinds; divyāni = divine; nānā-varṇa-ākṛtīni = of many colours and shapes; ca = and

11.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: पश्यादित्यान्वसून्रुद्रानश्विनौ मरुतस्तथा । / बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि पश्याश्चर्याणि भारत ॥
LIT: See the Ādityas, the Vasus, the Rudras, the two Aśvins and also the Maruts. See many wonders not seen before, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: paśya = see; ādityān = the Ādityas; vasūn = the Vasus; rudrān = the Rudras; aśvinau = the two Aśvins; marutaḥ = the Maruts; tathā = and also; bahūni = many; adṛṣṭa-pūrvāṇi = not seen before; paśya = see; āścaryāṇi = wonders; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata

11.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम् । / मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्चान्यद् द्रष्टुमिच्छसि ॥
LIT: See today the whole world, with the moving and the unmoving, standing in one place here in my body, O Guḍākeśa, and whatever else you wish to see.
WORDS: iha = here; eka-stham = standing in one place; jagat = the world; kṛtsnam = whole; paśya = see; adya = today; sa-cara-acaram = with the moving and the unmoving; mama = of me; dehe = in the body; guḍākeśa = O Guḍākeśa; yat = whatever; ca = and; anyat = else; draṣṭum = to see; icchasi = you wish

11.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न तु मां शक्यसे द्रष्टुमनेनैव स्वचक्षुषा । / दिव्यं ददामि ते चक्षुः पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ॥
LIT: But you are not able to see me with only this, your own eye. I give to you a divine eye. See my lordly yoga.
WORDS: na = not; tu = but; mām = me; śakyase = you are able; draṣṭum = to see; anena = with this; eva = only; sva-cakṣuṣā = with your own eye; divyam = divine; dadāmi = I give; te = to you; cakṣuḥ = an eye; paśya = see; me = of me; yogam = yoga; aiśvaram = lordly

11.9 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: एवमुक्त्वा ततो राजन्महायोगेश्वरो हरिः । / दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमं रूपमैश्वरम् ॥
LIT: Having spoken thus, O king, Hari, the great lord of yoga, then showed to the son of Pṛthā the highest lordly form,
WORDS: evam = thus; uktvā = having spoken; tataḥ = then; rājan = O king; mahā-yoga-īśvaraḥ = the great lord of yoga; hariḥ = Hari; darśayām āsa = showed; pārthāya = to the son of Pṛthā; paramam = highest; rūpam = form; aiśvaram = lordly
NOTE: Verses 9 to 11 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

11.10 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: अनेकवक्त्रनयनमनेकाद्भुतदर्शनम् । / अनेकदिव्याभरणं दिव्यानेकोद्यतायुधम् ॥
LIT: with many mouths and eyes, with many wondrous sights, with many divine ornaments, with many divine weapons raised,
WORDS: aneka-vaktra-nayanam = with many mouths and eyes; aneka-adbhuta-darśanam = with many wondrous sights; aneka-divya-ābharaṇam = with many divine ornaments; divya-aneka-udyata-āyudham = with many divine weapons raised

11.11 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् । / सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम् ॥
LIT: wearing divine garlands and garments, anointed with divine fragrances, made of all wonders, the god, endless, with faces on every side.
WORDS: divya-mālya-ambara-dharam = wearing divine garlands and garments; divya-gandha-anulepanam = anointed with divine fragrances; sarva-āścarya-mayam = made of all wonders; devam = the god; anantam = endless; viśvataḥ-mukham = with faces on every side

11.12 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेद्युगपदुत्थिता । / यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः ॥
LIT: If the light of a thousand suns were to be risen at once in the sky, that would be like the light of that one of great self.
WORDS: divi = in the sky; sūrya-sahasrasya = of a thousand suns; bhavet = were to be; yugapat = at once; utthitā = risen; yadi = if; bhāḥ = the light; sadṛśī = like; sā = that; syāt = would be; bhāsaḥ = the light; tasya = of that; mahā-ātmanaḥ = of the one of great self

11.13 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: तत्रैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा । / अपश्यद्देवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा ॥
LIT: There, in the body of the god of gods, the son of Pāṇḍu then saw the whole world standing in one place, divided in many ways.
WORDS: tatra = there; eka-stham = standing in one place; jagat = the world; kṛtsnam = whole; pravibhaktam = divided; anekadhā = in many ways; apaśyat = he saw; deva-devasya = of the god of gods; śarīre = in the body; pāṇḍavaḥ = the son of Pāṇḍu; tadā = then

11.14 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: ततः स विस्मयाविष्टो हृष्टरोमा धनञ्जयः । / प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं कृताञ्जलिरभाषत ॥
LIT: Then he, the winner of wealth, filled with amazement, with hair standing on end, having bowed with the head to the god, spoke with palms joined.
WORDS: tataḥ = then; saḥ = he; vismaya-āviṣṭaḥ = filled with amazement; hṛṣṭa-romā = with hair standing on end; dhanañjayaḥ = the winner of wealth; praṇamya = having bowed; śirasā = with the head; devam = to the god; kṛta-añjaliḥ = with palms joined; abhāṣata = spoke

11.15 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: पश्यामि देवांस्तव देव देहे / सर्वांस्तथा भूतविशेषसङ्घान् । / ब्रह्माणमीशं कमलासनस्थ- / मृषींश्च सर्वानुरगांश्च दिव्यान् ॥
LIT: I see all the gods in your body, O god, and also the hosts of the different kinds of beings, Brahmā the lord seated on the lotus seat, and all the seers and the divine serpents.
WORDS: paśyāmi = I see; devān = the gods; tava = of you; deva = O god; dehe = in the body; sarvān = all; tathā = and also; bhūta-viśeṣa-saṅghān = the hosts of the different kinds of beings; brahmāṇam = Brahmā; īśam = the lord; kamala-āsana-stham = seated on the lotus seat; ṛṣīn = the seers; ca = and; sarvān = all; uragān = the serpents; ca = and; divyān = divine
NOTE: "Brahmā the lord seated on the lotus seat" can also be read as two gods: "Brahmā seated on the lotus seat, and the Lord (Śiva)".

11.16 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: अनेकबाहूदरवक्त्रनेत्रं / पश्यामि त्वां सर्वतोऽनन्तरूपम् । / नान्तं न मध्यं न पुनस्तवादिं / पश्यामि विश्वेश्वर विश्वरूप ॥
LIT: I see you with many arms, bellies, mouths and eyes, of endless form on every side. I see no end, no middle, nor again a beginning of you, O lord of all, O one whose form is all.
WORDS: aneka-bāhu-udara-vaktra-netram = with many arms, bellies, mouths and eyes; paśyāmi = I see; tvām = you; sarvataḥ = on every side; ananta-rūpam = of endless form; na = not; antam = end; na = not; madhyam = middle; na = not; punaḥ = again; tava = of you; ādim = beginning; paśyāmi = I see; viśva-īśvara = O lord of all; viśva-rūpa = O one whose form is all

11.17 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च / तेजोराशिं सर्वतो दीप्तिमन्तम् । / पश्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्ताद् / दीप्तानलार्कद्युतिमप्रमेयम् ॥
LIT: I see you wearing a crown, bearing a mace and bearing a discus, a mass of radiance shining on every side, hard to look at, all around with the brightness of blazing fire and sun, immeasurable.
WORDS: kirīṭinam = wearing a crown; gadinam = bearing a mace; cakriṇam = bearing a discus; ca = and; tejaḥ-rāśim = a mass of radiance; sarvataḥ = on every side; dīptimantam = shining; paśyāmi = I see; tvām = you; durnirīkṣyam = hard to look at; samantāt = all around; dīpta-anala-arka-dyutim = with the brightness of blazing fire and sun; aprameyam = immeasurable

11.18 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: त्वमक्षरं परमं वेदितव्यं / त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् । / त्वमव्ययः शाश्वतधर्मगोप्ता / सनातनस्त्वं पुरुषो मतो मे ॥
LIT: You are the undecaying, the highest, to be known. You are the highest resting place of this all. You are the imperishable guardian of the everlasting dharma. You are the eternal person: so it is thought by me.
WORDS: tvam = you; akṣaram = the undecaying; paramam = highest; veditavyam = to be known; tvam = you; asya = of this; viśvasya = of all; param = highest; nidhānam = resting place; tvam = you; avyayaḥ = imperishable; śāśvata-dharma-goptā = guardian of the everlasting dharma; sanātanaḥ = eternal; tvam = you; puruṣaḥ = the person; mataḥ = thought; me = by me
NOTE: "The undecaying, the highest, to be known" can also be read as "the highest undecaying one that is to be known".

11.19 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्य- / मनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम् । / पश्यामि त्वां दीप्तहुताशवक्त्रं / स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम् ॥

11.20 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: द्यावापृथिव्योरिदमन्तरं हि / व्याप्तं त्वयैकेन दिशश्च सर्वाः । / दृष्ट्वाद्भुतं रूपमुग्रं तवेदं / लोकत्रयं प्रव्यथितं महात्मन् ॥

11.21 [Arjuna speaks]

11.22 [Arjuna speaks]

11.49 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढभावो / दृष्ट्वा रूपं घोरमीदृङ्ममेदम् । / व्यपेतभीः प्रीतमनाः पुनस्त्वं / तदेव मे रूपमिदं प्रपश्य ॥
LIT: Let there not be distress for you, and let there not be a bewildered state, having seen this form of mine, such a terrible one. With fear gone, with a glad mind, behold again that very form of mine, this one.
WORDS: mā = let there not be; te = for you; vyathā = distress; mā = let there not be; ca = and; vimūḍha-bhāvaḥ = a bewildered state; dṛṣṭvā = having seen; rūpam = form; ghoram = terrible; īdṛk = such; mama = of mine; idam = this; vyapeta-bhīḥ = with fear gone; prīta-manāḥ = with a glad mind; punaḥ = again; tvam = you; tat = that; eva = very; me = of mine; rūpam = form; idam = this; prapaśya = behold

11.50 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: इत्यर्जुनं वासुदेवस्तथोक्त्वा / स्वकं रूपं दर्शयामास भूयः । / आश्वासयामास च भीतमेनं / भूत्वा पुनः सौम्यवपुर्महात्मा ॥
LIT: Thus, having spoken so to Arjuna, Vāsudeva showed his own form once more. And the great self, having again become gentle in body, comforted him, the frightened one.
WORDS: iti = thus; arjunam = to Arjuna; vāsudevaḥ = Vāsudeva; tathā = so; uktvā = having spoken; svakam = his own; rūpam = form; darśayām āsa = he showed; bhūyaḥ = once more; āśvāsayām āsa = he comforted; ca = and; bhītam = frightened; enam = him; bhūtvā = having become; punaḥ = again; saumya-vapuḥ = gentle in body; mahā-ātmā = the great self

11.51 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: दृष्ट्वेदं मानुषं रूपं तव सौम्यं जनार्दन । / इदानीमस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिं गतः ॥
LIT: Having seen this gentle human form of yours, O Janārdana, I am now become in possession of my mind, gone to my own nature.
WORDS: dṛṣṭvā = having seen; idam = this; mānuṣam = human; rūpam = form; tava = of yours; saumyam = gentle; janārdana = O Janārdana; idānīm = now; asmi = I am; saṁvṛttaḥ = become; sa-cetāḥ = in possession of my mind; prakṛtim = to my own nature; gataḥ = gone

11.52 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सुदुर्दर्शमिदं रूपं दृष्टवानसि यन्मम । / देवा अप्यस्य रूपस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥
LIT: Very hard to see is this form of mine which you have seen. Even the gods are always longing for the sight of this form.
WORDS: su-dur-darśam = very hard to see; idam = this; rūpam = form; dṛṣṭavān = one who has seen; asi = you are; yat = which; mama = of mine; devāḥ = the gods; api = even; asya = of this; rūpasya = of the form; nityam = always; darśana-kāṅkṣiṇaḥ = longing for the sight

11.53 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया । / शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा ॥
LIT: Not by the Vedas, not by austerity, not by giving, and not by sacrifice am I able to be seen in such a way, as you have seen me.
WORDS: na = not; aham = I; vedaiḥ = by the Vedas; na = not; tapasā = by austerity; na = not; dānena = by giving; na = not; ca = and; ijyayā = by sacrifice; śakyaḥ = able; evaṁ-vidhaḥ = in such a way; draṣṭum = to be seen; dṛṣṭavān = one who has seen; asi = you are; mām = me; yathā = as

11.54 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: भक्त्या त्वनन्यया शक्य अहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन । / ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परन्तप ॥
LIT: But by devotion directed to no other, O Arjuna, I am able in such a way to be known and to be seen in truth, and to be entered, O scorcher of foes.
WORDS: bhaktyā = by devotion; tu = but; ananyayā = directed to no other; śakyaḥ = able; aham = I; evaṁ-vidhaḥ = in such a way; arjuna = O Arjuna; jñātum = to be known; draṣṭum = to be seen; ca = and; tattvena = in truth; praveṣṭum = to be entered; ca = and; parantapa = O scorcher of foes

11.55 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मत्कर्मकृन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः । / निर्वैरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव ॥
LIT: One who does action for me, holding me as the highest, devoted to me, free from attachment, who is without enmity toward all beings: he comes to me, O son of Pāṇḍu.
WORDS: mat-karma-kṛt = one who does action for me; mat-paramaḥ = holding me as the highest; mat-bhaktaḥ = devoted to me; saṅga-varjitaḥ = free from attachment; nirvairaḥ = without enmity; sarva-bhūteṣu = toward all beings; yaḥ = who; saḥ = he; mām = to me; eti = comes; pāṇḍava = O son of Pāṇḍu
NOTE: "One who does action for me" can also be read as "one who does my work".
