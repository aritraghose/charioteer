9.1 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे । / ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥
LIT: But to you, who do not find fault, I will declare this most secret knowledge, together with realization, having known which you will be freed from the inauspicious.
WORDS: idam = this; tu = but; te = to you; guhyatamam = most secret; pravakṣyāmi = I will declare; anasūyave = to one who does not find fault; jñānam = knowledge; vijñāna-sahitam = together with realization; yat = which; jñātvā = having known; mokṣyase = you will be freed; aśubhāt = from the inauspicious

9.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् । / प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम् ॥
LIT: This is the king of knowledge, the king of secrets, the highest purifier, understood by direct perception, in accord with dharma, very easy to do, imperishable.

NOTE: "The king of knowledge, the king of secrets" can also be read as "the knowledge of kings, the secret of kings".

SA: अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् । / साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः ॥

WORDS: api = even; cet = if; su-durācāraḥ = one of very bad conduct; bhajate = worships; mām = me; ananya-bhāk = devoted to no other; sādhuḥ = good; eva = indeed; saḥ = he; mantavyaḥ = to be thought of; samyak = rightly; vyavasitaḥ = resolved; hi = for; saḥ = he

9.31 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति । / कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति ॥
LIT: Quickly he becomes one whose self is dharma, and he reaches everlasting peace. O son of Kuntī, declare: my devotee does not perish.
WORDS: kṣipram = quickly; bhavati = he becomes; dharma-ātmā = one whose self is dharma; śaśvat = everlasting; śāntim = peace; nigacchati = he reaches; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; pratijānīhi = declare; na = not; me = my; bhaktaḥ = devotee; praṇaśyati = perishes
NOTE: "Declare" can also be read as "know for certain".

9.32 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः । / स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम् ॥
LIT: For having taken refuge in me, O son of Pṛthā, even those who may be of evil birth, women, merchants and also servants, they too go to the highest goal.
WORDS: mām = in me; hi = for; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; vyapāśritya = having taken refuge; ye = who; api = even; syuḥ = they may be; pāpa-yonayaḥ = of evil birth; striyaḥ = women; vaiśyāḥ = merchants; tathā = and also; śūdrāḥ = servants; te = they; api = too; yānti = they go; parām = highest; gatim = to the goal
NOTE: "Those who may be of evil birth, women, merchants and also servants" can be read as one group described by the three names that follow, or as four separate groups, with those of evil birth standing apart from the women, merchants and servants.

9.33 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा । / अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम् ॥
LIT: How much more then the pure brahmins, and also the devoted royal seers. Having come to this impermanent, joyless world, worship me.
WORDS: kim = how much; punaḥ = more then; brāhmaṇāḥ = brahmins; puṇyāḥ = pure; bhaktāḥ = devoted; rāja-ṛṣayaḥ = royal seers; tathā = and also; anityam = impermanent; asukham = joyless; lokam = world; imam = this; prāpya = having come to; bhajasva = worship; mām = me

9.34 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु । / मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥
LIT: Be with the mind on me, devoted to me, sacrificing to me. Bow to me. Having thus joined the self, having me as the highest goal, you will come to me alone.
WORDS: mat-manāḥ = with the mind on me; bhava = be; mat-bhaktaḥ = devoted to me; mat-yājī = sacrificing to me; mām = to me; namaskuru = bow; mām = to me; eva = alone; eṣyasi = you will come; yuktvā = having joined; evam = thus; ātmānam = the self; mat-parāyaṇaḥ = having me as the highest goal
