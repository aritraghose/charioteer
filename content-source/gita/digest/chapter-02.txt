2.1 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: तं तथा कृपयाविष्टमश्रुपूर्णाकुलेक्षणम् । / विषीदन्तमिदं वाक्यमुवाच मधुसूदनः ॥
LIT: To him, thus overcome by pity, whose eyes were filled with tears and troubled, despairing, the slayer of Madhu spoke this speech.
WORDS: tam = to him; tathā = thus; kṛpayā = by pity; āviṣṭam = overcome; aśru-pūrṇa-ākula-īkṣaṇam = whose eyes were filled with tears and troubled; viṣīdantam = despairing; idam = this; vākyam = speech; uvāca = spoke; madhusūdanaḥ = the slayer of Madhu

2.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कुतस्त्वा कश्मलमिदं विषमे समुपस्थितम् । / अनार्यजुष्टमस्वर्ग्यमकीर्तिकरमर्जुन ॥
LIT: From where has this faintness of spirit come upon you in difficulty, not practised by the noble, not leading to heaven, causing disgrace, O Arjuna?
WORDS: kutaḥ = from where; tvā = you; kaśmalam = faintness of spirit; idam = this; viṣame = in difficulty; samupasthitam = come upon; anārya-juṣṭam = not practised by the noble; asvargyam = not leading to heaven; akīrti-karam = causing disgrace; arjuna = O Arjuna

2.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते । / क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप ॥
LIT: Do not go to unmanliness at all, O son of Pṛthā. This is not fitting in you. Having abandoned petty weakness of heart, stand up, O scorcher of foes.
WORDS: klaibyam = to unmanliness; mā = do not; sma = at all; gamaḥ = go; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; na = not; etat = this; tvayi = in you; upapadyate = is fitting; kṣudram = petty; hṛdaya-daurbalyam = weakness of heart; tyaktvā = having abandoned; uttiṣṭha = stand up; parantapa = O scorcher of foes

2.4 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: कथं भीष्ममहं सङ्ख्ये द्रोणं च मधुसूदन । / इषुभिः प्रतियोत्स्यामि पूजार्हावरिसूदन ॥
LIT: How shall I fight back with arrows in battle against Bhīṣma and against Droṇa, O slayer of Madhu, the two worthy of worship, O slayer of enemies?
WORDS: katham = how; bhīṣmam = against Bhīṣma; aham = I; saṅkhye = in battle; droṇam = against Droṇa; ca = and; madhusūdana = O slayer of Madhu; iṣubhiḥ = with arrows; pratiyotsyāmi = shall I fight back; pūjā-arhau = the two worthy of worship; ari-sūdana = O slayer of enemies

2.5 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: गुरूनहत्वा हि महानुभावान् / श्रेयो भोक्तुं भैक्ष्यमपीह लोके । / हत्वार्थकामांस्तु गुरूनिहैव / भुञ्जीय भोगान् रुधिरप्रदिग्धान् ॥
LIT: For it is better to eat even begged food here in the world, not having slain the teachers of great dignity. But having slain the teachers, desirous of gain, I would eat right here enjoyments smeared with blood.
WORDS: gurūn = the teachers; ahatvā = not having slain; hi = for; mahā-anubhāvān = of great dignity; śreyaḥ = better; bhoktum = to eat; bhaikṣyam = begged food; api = even; iha = here; loke = in the world; hatvā = having slain; artha-kāmān = desirous of gain; tu = but; gurūn = the teachers; iha = here; eva = right; bhuñjīya = I would eat; bhogān = enjoyments; rudhira-pradigdhān = smeared with blood
NOTE: "Desirous of gain" is read here as describing the teachers. It can also describe the enjoyments: "enjoyments of wealth and pleasure, smeared with blood".

2.6 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: न चैतद्विद्मः कतरन्नो गरीयो / यद्वा जयेम यदि वा नो जयेयुः । / यानेव हत्वा न जिजीविषाम- / स्तेऽवस्थिताः प्रमुखे धार्तराष्ट्राः ॥
LIT: And we do not know this, which of the two is weightier for us: whether we should conquer, or if they should conquer us. The very ones whom having slain we do not wish to live, they are standing in front, the sons of Dhṛtarāṣṭra.
WORDS: na = not; ca = and; etat = this; vidmaḥ = we know; katarat = which of the two; naḥ = for us; garīyaḥ = weightier; yat = whether; vā = or; jayema = we should conquer; yadi = if; vā = or; naḥ = us; jayeyuḥ = they should conquer; yān = whom; eva = very; hatvā = having slain; na = not; jijīviṣāmaḥ = we wish to live; te = they; avasthitāḥ = standing; pramukhe = in front; dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ = the sons of Dhṛtarāṣṭra

2.7 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: कार्पण्यदोषोपहतस्वभावः / पृच्छामि त्वां धर्मसम्मूढचेताः । / यच्छ्रेयः स्यान्निश्चितं ब्रूहि तन्मे / शिष्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम् ॥
LIT: I, whose own nature is struck down by the fault of wretchedness, whose mind is confused about dharma, ask you: what would be better, tell that to me for certain. I am your pupil. Instruct me, who has taken refuge in you.
WORDS: kārpaṇya-doṣa-upahata-svabhāvaḥ = whose own nature is struck down by the fault of wretchedness; pṛcchāmi = I ask; tvām = you; dharma-sammūḍha-cetāḥ = whose mind is confused about dharma; yat = what; śreyaḥ = better; syāt = would be; niścitam = for certain; brūhi = tell; tat = that; me = to me; śiṣyaḥ = pupil; te = your; aham = I; śādhi = instruct; mām = me; tvām = in you; prapannam = who has taken refuge
NOTE: "For certain" is taken with "tell me". It can also go with "better": "tell me what is decidedly better".

2.8 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: न हि प्रपश्यामि ममापनुद्याद् / यच्छोकमुच्छोषणमिन्द्रियाणाम् । / अवाप्य भूमावसपत्नमृद्धं / राज्यं सुराणामपि चाधिपत्यम् ॥
LIT: For I do not see what would drive away my grief, the drying up of the senses, having obtained on earth a prosperous kingdom without rival, and even lordship of the gods.
WORDS: na = not; hi = for; prapaśyāmi = I see; mama = my; apanudyāt = would drive away; yat = what; śokam = grief; ucchoṣaṇam = drying up; indriyāṇām = of the senses; avāpya = having obtained; bhūmau = on earth; asapatnam = without rival; ṛddham = prosperous; rājyam = kingdom; surāṇām = of the gods; api = even; ca = and; ādhipatyam = lordship

2.9 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: एवमुक्त्वा हृषीकेशं गुडाकेशः परन्तप । / न योत्स्य इति गोविन्दमुक्त्वा तूष्णीं बभूव ह ॥
LIT: Having spoken thus to the lord of the senses, the lord of sleep, O scorcher of foes, having said to Govinda thus: "I will not fight", became silent indeed.
WORDS: evam = thus; uktvā = having spoken; hṛṣīkeśam = to the lord of the senses; guḍākeśaḥ = the lord of sleep; parantapa = O scorcher of foes; na = not; yotsye = I will fight; iti = thus; govindam = to Govinda; uktvā = having said; tūṣṇīm = silent; babhūva = became; ha = indeed
NOTE: "O scorcher of foes" follows this text, where Sañjaya addresses the king. Other copies of the text read it as describing Arjuna: "the scorcher of foes fell silent".

2.10 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: तमुवाच हृषीकेशः प्रहसन्निव भारत । / सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये विषीदन्तमिदं वचः ॥
LIT: To him, despairing in the middle of both of the two armies, O descendant of Bharata, the lord of the senses, as if smiling, spoke this word.
WORDS: tam = to him; uvāca = spoke; hṛṣīkeśaḥ = the lord of the senses; prahasan = smiling; iva = as if; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata; senayoḥ = of the two armies; ubhayoḥ = of both; madhye = in the middle; viṣīdantam = despairing; idam = this; vacaḥ = word

2.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अशोच्यानन्वशोचस्त्वं प्रज्ञावादांश्च भाषसे । / गतासूनगतासूंश्च नानुशोचन्ति पण्डिताः ॥
LIT: You have grieved for those not to be grieved for, and you speak words of wisdom. The learned do not grieve for those whose breath has gone and those whose breath has not gone.
WORDS: aśocyān = those not to be grieved for; anvaśocaḥ = have grieved for; tvam = you; prajñā-vādān = words of wisdom; ca = and; bhāṣase = you speak; gata-asūn = those whose breath has gone; agata-asūn = those whose breath has not gone; ca = and; na = not; anuśocanti = grieve for; paṇḍitāḥ = the learned

2.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः । / न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम् ॥
LIT: But indeed it is not that I ever was not, nor you, nor these lords of men. And indeed it is not that we all shall not be, from this onward.
WORDS: na = not; tu = but; eva = indeed; aham = I; jātu = ever; na = not; āsam = was; na = nor; tvam = you; na = nor; ime = these; jana-adhipāḥ = lords of men; na = not; ca = and; eva = indeed; na = not; bhaviṣyāmaḥ = shall be; sarve = all; vayam = we; ataḥ = from this; param = onward

2.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: देहिनोऽस्मिन्यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा । / तथा देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति ॥
LIT: Just as childhood, youth and old age are of the embodied one in this body, so is the attaining of another body. The steadfast one is not confused about that.
WORDS: dehinaḥ = of the embodied one; asmin = in this; yathā = just as; dehe = in the body; kaumāram = childhood; yauvanam = youth; jarā = old age; tathā = so; deha-antara-prāptiḥ = the attaining of another body; dhīraḥ = the steadfast one; tatra = about that; na = not; muhyati = is confused
NOTE: The word translated "steadfast" (dhīra) can equally mean "wise".

2.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः । / आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत ॥
LIT: The contacts of the senses with their objects, O son of Kuntī, give cold and heat, pleasure and pain. They come and go and do not last. Endure them, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: mātrā-sparśāḥ = the contacts of the senses with their objects; tu = but; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha-dāḥ = givers of cold and heat, pleasure and pain; āgama-apāyinaḥ = coming and going; anityāḥ = not lasting; tān = them; titikṣasva = endure; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata

2.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यं हि न व्यथयन्त्येते पुरुषं पुरुषर्षभ । / समदुःखसुखं धीरं सोऽमृतत्वाय कल्पते ॥
LIT: For the man whom these do not disturb, O bull among men, the same in pain and pleasure, steadfast, he is fit for deathlessness.
WORDS: yam = whom; hi = for; na = not; vyathayanti = disturb; ete = these; puruṣam = the man; puruṣa-ṛṣabha = O bull among men; sama-duḥkha-sukham = the same in pain and pleasure; dhīram = steadfast; saḥ = he; amṛtatvāya = for deathlessness; kalpate = is fit
NOTE: The word translated "steadfast" (dhīra) can equally mean "wise".

2.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः । / उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ॥
LIT: Being is not found of what is not. Non-being is not found of what is. But the end of even both of these two is seen by the seers of the truth.
WORDS: na = not; asataḥ = of what is not; vidyate = is found; bhāvaḥ = being; na = not; abhāvaḥ = non-being; vidyate = is found; sataḥ = of what is; ubhayoḥ = of both; api = even; dṛṣṭaḥ = seen; antaḥ = the end; tu = but; anayoḥ = of these two; tattva-darśibhiḥ = by the seers of the truth
NOTE: "The end" can also be read as "the conclusion": the settled truth about what is and what is not.

2.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अविनाशि तु तद्विद्धि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् । / विनाशमव्ययस्यास्य न कश्चित्कर्तुमर्हति ॥
LIT: But know that to be indestructible, by which all this is pervaded. Not anyone is able to bring about the destruction of this imperishable.
WORDS: avināśi = indestructible; tu = but; tat = that; viddhi = know; yena = by which; sarvam = all; idam = this; tatam = pervaded; vināśam = the destruction; avyayasya = of the imperishable; asya = of this; na = not; kaścit = anyone; kartum = to bring about; arhati = is able

2.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अन्तवन्त इमे देहा नित्यस्योक्ताः शरीरिणः । / अनाशिनोऽप्रमेयस्य तस्माद्युध्यस्व भारत ॥
LIT: These bodies of the eternal embodied one, of the indestructible, of the immeasurable, are said to be having an end. Therefore fight, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: antavantaḥ = having an end; ime = these; dehāḥ = bodies; nityasya = of the eternal; uktāḥ = are said; śarīriṇaḥ = of the embodied one; anāśinaḥ = of the indestructible; aprameyasya = of the immeasurable; tasmāt = therefore; yudhyasva = fight; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata

2.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: य एनं वेत्ति हन्तारं यश्चैनं मन्यते हतम् । / उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं हन्ति न हन्यते ॥
LIT: He who knows this one as the slayer, and he who thinks this one slain, both of those two do not understand. This one does not slay and is not slain.
WORDS: yaḥ = he who; enam = this one; vetti = knows; hantāram = as the slayer; yaḥ = he who; ca = and; enam = this one; manyate = thinks; hatam = slain; ubhau = both; tau = those two; na = not; vijānītaḥ = they two understand; na = not; ayam = this one; hanti = slays; na = not; hanyate = is slain

2.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन् / नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः । / अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो / न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥
LIT: This one is not born, or dies, at any time. Nor, having been, will this one again not be. Unborn, eternal, everlasting, ancient, this one is not slain when the body is being slain.
WORDS: na = not; jāyate = is born; mriyate = dies; vā = or; kadācit = at any time; na = not; ayam = this one; bhūtvā = having been; bhavitā = will be; vā = or; na = not; bhūyaḥ = again; ajaḥ = unborn; nityaḥ = eternal; śāśvataḥ = everlasting; ayam = this one; purāṇaḥ = ancient; na = not; hanyate = is slain; hanyamāne = being slain; śarīre = when the body
NOTE: The second line has several negatives and can be divided differently, giving "having come to be, it will not come to be again".

2.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: वेदाविनाशिनं नित्यं य एनमजमव्ययम् । / कथं स पुरुषः पार्थ कं घातयति हन्ति कम् ॥
LIT: He who knows this one as indestructible, eternal, unborn, undecaying: how, O son of Pṛthā, does that person cause whom to be slain, and whom does he slay?
WORDS: veda = knows; avināśinam = indestructible; nityam = eternal; yaḥ = he who; enam = this one; ajam = unborn; avyayam = undecaying; katham = how; saḥ = that; puruṣaḥ = person; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; kam = whom; ghātayati = causes to be slain; hanti = slays; kam = whom
NOTE: The last part is two literal questions. It is usually understood as one: "how can that person slay anyone, or cause anyone to be slain?"

2.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय / नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि । / तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णा- / न्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥

2.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नैनं छिन्दन्ति शस्त्राणि नैनं दहति पावकः । / न चैनं क्लेदयन्त्यापो न शोषयति मारुतः ॥

2.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अच्छेद्योऽयमदाह्योऽयमक्लेद्योऽशोष्य एव च । / नित्यः सर्वगतः स्थाणुरचलोऽयं सनातनः ॥

2.25 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अव्यक्तोऽयमचिन्त्योऽयमविकार्योऽयमुच्यते । / तस्मादेवं विदित्वैनं नानुशोचितुमर्हसि ॥

2.26 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अथ चैनं नित्यजातं नित्यं वा मन्यसे मृतम् । / तथापि त्वं महाबाहो नैवं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥

2.27 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्ध्रुवं जन्म मृतस्य च । / तस्मादपरिहार्येऽर्थे न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥

2.28 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

2.59 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

2.61 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

2.62 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

2.63 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
