14.1 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: परं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि ज्ञानानां ज्ञानमुत्तमम् । / यज्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः ॥
LIT: Again I shall declare the supreme knowledge, the highest of knowledges, having known which all the sages have gone from here to supreme perfection.
WORDS: param = supreme; bhūyaḥ = again; pravakṣyāmi = I shall declare; jñānānām = of knowledges; jñānam = knowledge; uttamam = the highest; yat = which; jñātvā = having known; munayaḥ = the sages; sarve = all; parām = supreme; siddhim = to perfection; itaḥ = from here; gatāḥ = gone
NOTE: "Again I shall declare the supreme knowledge" can also be read as "I shall declare further, once again, the knowledge", with the first word meaning "further" rather than "supreme".

14.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रित्य मम साधर्म्यमागताः । / सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च ॥
LIT: Having resorted to this knowledge and having come to sameness of nature with me, they are not born even at creation, and at dissolution they are not disturbed.
WORDS: idam = this; jñānam = knowledge; upāśritya = having resorted to; mama = of me; sādharmyam = to sameness of nature; āgatāḥ = having come; sarge = at creation; api = even; na = not; upajāyante = they are born; pralaye = at dissolution; na = not; vyathanti = they are disturbed; ca = and

14.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मम योनिर्महद् ब्रह्म तस्मिन्गर्भं दधाम्यहम् । / सम्भवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत ॥
LIT: My womb is the great brahman. In that I place the embryo. From that comes the coming into being of all beings, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: mama = my; yoniḥ = the womb; mahat = great; brahma = brahman; tasmin = in that; garbham = the embryo; dadhāmi = I place; aham = I; sambhavaḥ = the coming into being; sarva-bhūtānām = of all beings; tataḥ = from that; bhavati = comes to be; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata

14.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः । / तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता ॥
LIT: Whichever forms come into being in all wombs, O son of Kuntī, of them the great brahman is the womb, and I am the seed-giving father.
WORDS: sarva-yoniṣu = in all wombs; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; mūrtayaḥ = forms; sambhavanti = come into being; yāḥ = whichever; tāsām = of them; brahma = brahman; mahat = great; yoniḥ = the womb; aham = I; bīja-pradaḥ = seed-giving; pitā = the father

14.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसम्भवाः । / निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥
LIT: Sattva, rajas and tamas: thus the guṇas, born of nature, bind the imperishable embodied one in the body, O mighty-armed one.
WORDS: sattvam = sattva; rajaḥ = rajas; tamaḥ = tamas; iti = thus; guṇāḥ = the guṇas; prakṛti-sambhavāḥ = born of nature; nibadhnanti = they bind; mahā-bāho = O mighty-armed one; dehe = in the body; dehinam = the embodied one; avyayam = imperishable

14.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् । / सुखसङ्गेन बध्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ ॥
LIT: There, sattva, illuminating and free from ill from its stainlessness, binds by attachment to happiness and by attachment to knowledge, O sinless one.
WORDS: tatra = there; sattvam = sattva; nirmalatvāt = from stainlessness; prakāśakam = illuminating; anāmayam = free from ill; sukha-saṅgena = by attachment to happiness; badhnāti = it binds; jñāna-saṅgena = by attachment to knowledge; ca = and; anagha = O sinless one

14.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् । / तन्निबध्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम् ॥
LIT: Know rajas as having the nature of passion, arising from thirst and attachment. That binds the embodied one, O son of Kuntī, by attachment to action.
WORDS: rajaḥ = rajas; rāga-ātmakam = having the nature of passion; viddhi = know; tṛṣṇā-saṅga-samudbhavam = arising from thirst and attachment; tat = that; nibadhnāti = binds; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; karma-saṅgena = by attachment to action; dehinam = the embodied one
NOTE: "Arising from thirst and attachment" can also be read as "the source of thirst and attachment", so that rajas produces them rather than comes from them.

14.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तमस्त्वज्ञानजं विद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम् । / प्रमादालस्यनिद्राभिस्तन्निबध्नाति भारत ॥
LIT: But know tamas as born of ignorance, the deluding of all embodied ones. That binds by heedlessness, laziness and sleep, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: tamaḥ = tamas; tu = but; ajñāna-jam = born of ignorance; viddhi = know; mohanam = the deluding; sarva-dehinām = of all embodied ones; pramāda-ālasya-nidrābhiḥ = by heedlessness, laziness and sleep; tat = that; nibadhnāti = binds; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata

14.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति रजः कर्मणि भारत । / ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे सञ्जयत्युत ॥
LIT: Sattva attaches one to happiness, rajas to action, O descendant of Bharata, but tamas, having veiled knowledge, attaches one to heedlessness indeed.
WORDS: sattvam = sattva; sukhe = to happiness; sañjayati = it attaches; rajaḥ = rajas; karmaṇi = to action; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata; jñānam = knowledge; āvṛtya = having veiled; tu = but; tamaḥ = tamas; pramāde = to heedlessness; sañjayati = it attaches; uta = indeed

14.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत । / रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा ॥
LIT: Having overpowered rajas and tamas, sattva comes to be, O descendant of Bharata. Rajas indeed comes to be having overpowered sattva and tamas, and likewise tamas having overpowered sattva and rajas.
WORDS: rajaḥ = rajas; tamaḥ = tamas; ca = and; abhibhūya = having overpowered; sattvam = sattva; bhavati = comes to be; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata; rajaḥ = rajas; sattvam = sattva; tamaḥ = tamas; ca = and; eva = indeed; tamaḥ = tamas; sattvam = sattva; rajaḥ = rajas; tathā = likewise

14.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्वद्वारेषु देहेऽस्मिन्प्रकाश उपजायते । / ज्ञानं यदा तदा विद्याद्विवृद्धं सत्त्वमित्युत ॥
LIT: When light, which is knowledge, is born in all the gates in this body, then indeed one should know thus: sattva has increased.
WORDS: sarva-dvāreṣu = in all the gates; dehe = in the body; asmin = in this; prakāśaḥ = light; upajāyate = is born; jñānam = knowledge; yadā = when; tadā = then; vidyāt = one should know; vivṛddham = increased; sattvam = sattva; iti = thus; uta = indeed

14.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: लोभः प्रवृत्तिरारम्भः कर्मणामशमः स्पृहा । / रजस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे भरतर्षभ ॥
LIT: Greed, activity, the undertaking of actions, restlessness, longing: these are born when rajas has increased, O bull of the Bharatas.
WORDS: lobhaḥ = greed; pravṛttiḥ = activity; ārambhaḥ = the undertaking; karmaṇām = of actions; aśamaḥ = restlessness; spṛhā = longing; rajasi = when rajas; etāni = these; jāyante = are born; vivṛddhe = has increased; bharata-ṛṣabha = O bull of the Bharatas

14.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च । / तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे कुरुनन्दन ॥
LIT: Absence of light and inactivity, heedlessness and indeed delusion: these are born when tamas has increased, O joy of the Kurus.
WORDS: aprakāśaḥ = absence of light; apravṛttiḥ = inactivity; ca = and; pramādaḥ = heedlessness; mohaḥ = delusion; eva = indeed; ca = and; tamasi = when tamas; etāni = these; jāyante = are born; vivṛddhe = has increased; kuru-nandana = O joy of the Kurus

14.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु प्रलयं याति देहभृत् । / तदोत्तमविदां लोकानमलान्प्रतिपद्यते ॥
LIT: But when the bearer of a body goes to dissolution when sattva has grown strong, then he reaches the stainless worlds of the knowers of the highest.
WORDS: yadā = when; sattve = when sattva; pravṛddhe = has grown strong; tu = but; pralayam = to dissolution; yāti = goes; deha-bhṛt = the bearer of a body; tadā = then; uttama-vidām = of the knowers of the highest; lokān = the worlds; amalān = stainless; pratipadyate = he reaches

14.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: रजसि प्रलयं गत्वा कर्मसङ्गिषु जायते । / तथा प्रलीनस्तमसि मूढयोनिषु जायते ॥
LIT: Having gone to dissolution in rajas, he is born among those attached to action. Likewise, dissolved in tamas, he is born in the wombs of the deluded.
WORDS: rajasi = in rajas; pralayam = to dissolution; gatvā = having gone; karma-saṅgiṣu = among those attached to action; jāyate = he is born; tathā = likewise; pralīnaḥ = dissolved; tamasi = in tamas; mūḍha-yoniṣu = in the wombs of the deluded; jāyate = he is born

14.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम् । / रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम् ॥
LIT: They say the fruit of action well done is of the nature of sattva and stainless, but the fruit of rajas is sorrow, and the fruit of tamas is ignorance.
WORDS: karmaṇaḥ = of action; sukṛtasya = well done; āhuḥ = they say; sāttvikam = of the nature of sattva; nirmalam = stainless; phalam = the fruit; rajasaḥ = of rajas; tu = but; phalam = the fruit; duḥkham = sorrow; ajñānam = ignorance; tamasaḥ = of tamas; phalam = the fruit

14.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सत्त्वात्सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च । / प्रमादमोहौ तमसो भवतोऽज्ञानमेव च ॥
LIT: From sattva knowledge is born, and from rajas indeed greed. Heedlessness and delusion come to be from tamas, and indeed ignorance.
WORDS: sattvāt = from sattva; sañjāyate = is born; jñānam = knowledge; rajasaḥ = from rajas; lobhaḥ = greed; eva = indeed; ca = and; pramāda-mohau = heedlessness and delusion; tamasaḥ = from tamas; bhavataḥ = the two come to be; ajñānam = ignorance; eva = indeed; ca = and

14.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्वस्था मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः । / जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अधो गच्छन्ति तामसाः ॥
LIT: Those standing in sattva go upward. Those of rajas stay in the middle. Those of tamas, standing in the workings of the lowest guṇa, go downward.
WORDS: ūrdhvam = upward; gacchanti = they go; sattva-sthāḥ = those standing in sattva; madhye = in the middle; tiṣṭhanti = they stay; rājasāḥ = those of rajas; jaghanya-guṇa-vṛtti-sthāḥ = standing in the workings of the lowest guṇa; adhaḥ = downward; gacchanti = they go; tāmasāḥ = those of tamas

14.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नान्यं गुणेभ्यः कर्तारं यदा द्रष्टानुपश्यति । / गुणेभ्यश्च परं वेत्ति मद्भावं सोऽधिगच्छति ॥
LIT: When the seer sees no doer other than the guṇas, and knows what is higher than the guṇas, he reaches my state of being.
WORDS: na = not; anyam = other; guṇebhyaḥ = than the guṇas; kartāram = a doer; yadā = when; draṣṭā = the seer; anupaśyati = sees; guṇebhyaḥ = than the guṇas; ca = and; param = what is higher; vetti = he knows; mat-bhāvam = my state of being; saḥ = he; adhigacchati = reaches

14.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान् । / जन्ममृत्युजरादुःखैर्विमुक्तोऽमृतमश्नुते ॥
LIT: Having gone beyond these three guṇas, arising from the body, the embodied one, freed from the sorrows of birth, death and old age, attains the deathless.
WORDS: guṇān = the guṇas; etān = these; atītya = having gone beyond; trīn = three; dehī = the embodied one; deha-samudbhavān = arising from the body; janma-mṛtyu-jarā-duḥkhaiḥ = from the sorrows of birth, death and old age; vimuktaḥ = freed; amṛtam = the deathless; aśnute = he attains
NOTE: "Arising from the body" can also be read as "from which the body arises", so that the guṇas are the source of the body rather than the reverse.
NOTE: "From the sorrows of birth, death and old age" can also be read as "from birth, death, old age and sorrow", four separate things.

14.21 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: कैर्लिङ्गैस्त्रीन्गुणानेतानतीतो भवति प्रभो । / किमाचारः कथं चैतांस्त्रीन्गुणानतिवर्तते ॥
LIT: By what marks is he one who has gone beyond these three guṇas, O lord? Of what conduct is he, and how does he pass beyond these three guṇas?
WORDS: kaiḥ = by what; liṅgaiḥ = by marks; trīn = three; guṇān = the guṇas; etān = these; atītaḥ = one who has gone beyond; bhavati = he is; prabho = O lord; kim-ācāraḥ = of what conduct; katham = how; ca = and; etān = these; trīn = three; guṇān = the guṇas; ativartate = he passes beyond

14.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव । / न द्वेष्टि सम्प्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति ॥
LIT: Light and activity and indeed delusion, O son of Pāṇḍu: he does not hate them when they have come forth, and does not long for them when they have ceased.
WORDS: prakāśam = light; ca = and; pravṛttim = activity; ca = and; moham = delusion; eva = indeed; ca = and; pāṇḍava = O son of Pāṇḍu; na = not; dveṣṭi = he hates; sampravṛttāni = when they have come forth; na = not; nivṛttāni = when they have ceased; kāṅkṣati = he longs for

14.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: उदासीनवदासीनो गुणैर्यो न विचाल्यते । / गुणा वर्तन्त इत्येवं योऽवतिष्ठति नेङ्गते ॥
LIT: He who, seated like one unconcerned, is not shaken by the guṇas, who stands firm thus, "the guṇas act", and does not waver,
WORDS: udāsīna-vat = like one unconcerned; āsīnaḥ = seated; guṇaiḥ = by the guṇas; yaḥ = who; na = not; vicālyate = is shaken; guṇāḥ = the guṇas; vartante = they act; iti = so; evam = thus; yaḥ = who; avatiṣṭhati = stands firm; na = not; iṅgate = he wavers
NOTE: Verses 23 to 25 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

14.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: समदुःखसुखः स्वस्थः समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः । / तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः ॥
LIT: who is the same in sorrow and happiness, abiding in himself, to whom a clod, a stone and gold are the same, to whom the dear and the not dear are equal, who is steady, to whom blame and praise of himself are equal,

14.25 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मानापमानयोस्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः । / सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते ॥

14.26 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

14.27 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ब्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च । / शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्यैकान्तिकस्य च ॥

NOTE: "Of brahman, of the deathless and the imperishable" can also be read as "of the deathless and imperishable brahman", with both words describing brahman rather than naming something separate.
