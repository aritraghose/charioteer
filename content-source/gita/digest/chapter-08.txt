8.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: किं तद् ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम । / अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥
LIT: What is that brahman? What is that which concerns the self? What is action, O highest of persons? And what is declared to be that which concerns beings? What is called that which concerns the gods?
WORDS: kim = what; tat = that; brahma = brahman; kim = what; adhyātmam = what concerns the self; kim = what; karma = action; puruṣa-uttama = O highest of persons; adhibhūtam = what concerns beings; ca = and; kim = what; proktam = declared; adhidaivam = what concerns the gods; kim = what; ucyate = is called

8.2 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन । / प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥
LIT: Who is that which concerns sacrifice, and how is it here in this body, O slayer of Madhu? And how are you to be known at the time of departure by those of controlled self?
WORDS: adhiyajñaḥ = what concerns sacrifice; katham = how; kaḥ = who; atra = here; dehe = in the body; asmin = in this; madhu-sūdana = O slayer of Madhu; prayāṇa-kāle = at the time of departure; ca = and; katham = how; jñeyaḥ = to be known; asi = you are; niyata-ātmabhiḥ = by those of controlled self

8.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते । / भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥
LIT: Brahman is the imperishable, the highest. One's own nature is called that which concerns the self. The sending forth that causes the arising of the states of beings is named action.
WORDS: akṣaram = the imperishable; brahma = brahman; paramam = the highest; svabhāvaḥ = one's own nature; adhyātmam = what concerns the self; ucyate = is called; bhūta-bhāva-udbhava-karaḥ = causing the arising of the states of beings; visargaḥ = the sending forth; karma-saṁjñitaḥ = named action
NOTE: "The sending forth" can also be read as "the offering", meaning the act of giving in sacrifice.

8.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् । / अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥
LIT: That which concerns beings is perishable existence, and the person is that which concerns the gods. That which concerns sacrifice is I alone, here in the body, O best of those who bear a body.
WORDS: adhibhūtam = what concerns beings; kṣaraḥ = perishable; bhāvaḥ = existence; puruṣaḥ = the person; ca = and; adhidaivatam = what concerns the gods; adhiyajñaḥ = what concerns sacrifice; aham = I; eva = alone; atra = here; dehe = in the body; deha-bhṛtām = of those who bear a body; vara = O best

8.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् । / यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥
LIT: And he who departs at the time of the end, remembering me alone, having let go of the body, goes to my state of being. There is no doubt here.
WORDS: anta-kāle = at the time of the end; ca = and; mām = me; eva = alone; smaran = remembering; muktvā = having let go of; kalevaram = the body; yaḥ = who; prayāti = departs; saḥ = he; mad-bhāvam = to my state of being; yāti = goes; na = not; asti = there is; atra = here; saṁśayaḥ = doubt

8.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् । / तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥
LIT: Or also, whichever state of being one remembers when one gives up the body at the end, to that very one one goes, O son of Kuntī, always made to become that state of being.
WORDS: yam = whichever; yam = whichever; vā = or; api = also; smaran = remembering; bhāvam = state of being; tyajati = one gives up; ante = at the end; kalevaram = the body; tam = to that; tam = to that; eva = very; eti = one goes; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; sadā = always; tad-bhāva-bhāvitaḥ = made to become that state of being

8.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च । / मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयः ॥
LIT: Therefore at all times remember me and fight. With mind and buddhi fixed on me, you will come to me alone, without doubt.

NOTE: "Without doubt" can be read either as "it is certain that you will come" or as "you, being free of doubt, will come".

8.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना । / परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन् ॥

8.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कविं पुराणमनुशासितार- / मणोरणीयंसमनुस्मरेद्यः । / सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूप- / मादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥
LIT: He who would remember the seer, the ancient one, the ruler, smaller than the small, the supporter of all, of unthinkable form, of the colour of the sun, beyond darkness,
WORDS: kavim = the seer; purāṇam = the ancient one; anuśāsitāram = the ruler; aṇoḥ = than the small; aṇīyāṁsam = smaller; anusmaret = would remember; yaḥ = who; sarvasya = of all; dhātāram = the supporter; acintya-rūpam = of unthinkable form; āditya-varṇam = of the colour of the sun; tamasaḥ = of darkness; parastāt = beyond
NOTE: Verses 9 to 10 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

8.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रयाणकाले मनसाऽचलेन / भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव । / भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्यक् / स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥
LIT: at the time of departure, with unmoving mind, yoked with devotion and indeed with the strength of yoga, having rightly made the breath enter in the middle of the two eyebrows, he goes to that highest divine person.
WORDS: prayāṇa-kāle = at the time of departure; manasā = with mind; acalena = unmoving; bhaktyā = with devotion; yuktaḥ = yoked; yoga-balena = with the strength of yoga; ca = and; eva = indeed; bhruvoḥ = of the two eyebrows; madhye = in the middle; prāṇam = the breath; āveśya = having made enter; samyak = rightly; saḥ = he; tam = that; param = highest; puruṣam = to the person; upaiti = goes; divyam = divine

8.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति / विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः । / यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति / तत्ते पदं सङ्ग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये ॥
LIT: That which knowers of the Vedas speak of as the imperishable, which strivers whose passion is gone enter, desiring which they live the life of a student of brahman: that state I will declare to you in brief.
WORDS: yat = which; akṣaram = the imperishable; veda-vidaḥ = knowers of the Vedas; vadanti = they speak of; viśanti = they enter; yat = which; yatayaḥ = strivers; vīta-rāgāḥ = whose passion is gone; yat = which; icchantaḥ = desiring; brahma-caryam = the life of a student of brahman; caranti = they live; tat = that; te = to you; padam = state; saṅgraheṇa = in brief; pravakṣye = I will declare
NOTE: "That state" can also be read as "that word", pointing ahead to the syllable Om in verse 13.

8.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य मनो हृदि निरुध्य च । / मूर्ध्न्याधायात्मनः प्राणमास्थितो योगधारणाम् ॥
LIT: Having restrained all the gates and having confined the mind in the heart, having placed the breath of the self in the head, established in the holding of yoga,

NOTE: Verses 12 to 13 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

8.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् । / यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥
LIT: uttering thus "Om", the brahman of one syllable, remembering me, he who departs, giving up the body, goes to the highest goal.
WORDS: om = Om; iti = thus; eka-akṣaram = of one syllable; brahma = brahman; vyāharan = uttering; mām = me; anusmaran = remembering; yaḥ = who; prayāti = departs; tyajan = giving up; deham = the body; saḥ = he; yāti = goes; paramām = to the highest; gatim = goal
NOTE: "The brahman of one syllable" can also be read as "the one imperishable brahman".

8.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः । / तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः ॥

WORDS: ananya-cetāḥ = with a mind on no other; satatam = continually; yaḥ = who; mām = me; smarati = remembers; nityaśaḥ = always; tasya = for him; aham = I; sulabhaḥ = easy to reach; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; nitya-yuktasya = for the ever-yoked; yoginaḥ = for the yogī

8.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् । / नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः ॥

8.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन । / मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥
LIT: The worlds, up to the realm of Brahmā, return again, O Arjuna. But having come to me, O son of Kuntī, there is no rebirth.
WORDS: ā-brahma-bhuvanāt = up to the realm of Brahmā; lokāḥ = the worlds; punaḥ-āvartinaḥ = returning again; arjuna = O Arjuna; mām = to me; upetya = having come; tu = but; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; punar-janma = rebirth; na = not; vidyate = there is
NOTE: "The worlds, up to the realm of Brahmā, return again" can also be read as "the worlds, up to the realm of Brahmā, are places from which one returns again".

8.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद् ब्रह्मणो विदुः । / रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ॥

8.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अव्यक्ताद् व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे । / रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके ॥

WORDS: avyaktāt = from the unmanifest; vyaktayaḥ = manifest things; sarvāḥ = all; prabhavanti = they come forth; ahaḥ-āgame = at the coming of the day; rātri-āgame = at the coming of the night; pralīyante = they dissolve; tatra = into that; eva = very; avyakta-saṁjñake = into what is named the unmanifest

8.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते । / रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे ॥

WORDS: bhūta-grāmaḥ = the multitude of beings; saḥ = that; eva = very same; ayam = this; bhūtvā = having come to be; bhūtvā = having come to be; pralīyate = it dissolves; rātri-āgame = at the coming of the night; avaśaḥ = without will; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; prabhavati = it comes forth; ahaḥ-āgame = at the coming of the day

8.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: परस्तस्मात्तु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः । / यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नश्यत्सु न विनश्यति ॥

8.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् । / यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥

8.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया । / यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥
