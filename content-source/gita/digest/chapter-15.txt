15.1 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम् । / छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित् ॥
LIT: They speak of the undecaying sacred fig tree with roots above and with branches below, of which the leaves are the sacred hymns. He who knows it, he is a knower of the Vedas.
WORDS: ūrdhva-mūlam = with roots above; adhaḥ-śākham = with branches below; aśvattham = the sacred fig tree; prāhuḥ = they speak of; avyayam = undecaying; chandāṁsi = the sacred hymns; yasya = of which; parṇāni = the leaves; yaḥ = he who; tam = it; veda = knows; saḥ = he; veda-vit = a knower of the Vedas

15.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अधश्चोर्ध्वं प्रसृतास्तस्य शाखा / गुणप्रवृद्धा विषयप्रवालाः । / अधश्च मूलान्यनुसन्ततानि / कर्मानुबन्धीनि मनुष्यलोके ॥
LIT: Downward and upward the branches of it are spread out, grown strong by the guṇas, with the objects of sense as shoots. And downward the roots are stretched out, bound up with action, in the world of men.
WORDS: adhaḥ = downward; ca = and; ūrdhvam = upward; prasṛtāḥ = spread out; tasya = of it; śākhāḥ = the branches; guṇa-pravṛddhāḥ = grown strong by the guṇas; viṣaya-pravālāḥ = with the objects of sense as shoots; adhaḥ = downward; ca = and; mūlāni = the roots; anusantatāni = stretched out; karma-anubandhīni = bound up with action; manuṣya-loke = in the world of men
NOTE: "Bound up with action" can also be read as "resulting in action".

15.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते / नान्तो न चादिर्न च सम्प्रतिष्ठा । / अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूलं / असङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा ॥
LIT: The form of it is not perceived here in that way, nor the end, nor the beginning, nor the foundation. Having cut this sacred fig tree, whose roots are fully grown, with the firm weapon of non-attachment,
WORDS: na = not; rūpam = the form; asya = of it; iha = here; tathā = in that way; upalabhyate = is perceived; na = not; antaḥ = the end; na = not; ca = and; ādiḥ = the beginning; na = not; ca = and; sampratiṣṭhā = the foundation; aśvattham = the sacred fig tree; enam = this; su-virūḍha-mūlam = whose roots are fully grown; asaṅga-śastreṇa = with the weapon of non-attachment; dṛḍhena = firm; chittvā = having cut
NOTE: Verses 3 to 4 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

15.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं / यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः । / तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये । / यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी ॥
LIT: then that place is to be sought, to which those who have gone do not return again. And I take refuge in that very primal person, from whom the ancient activity has flowed forth.
WORDS: tataḥ = then; padam = place; tat = that; parimārgitavyam = is to be sought; yasmin = to which; gatāḥ = those who have gone; na = not; nivartanti = they return; bhūyaḥ = again; tam = in that; eva = very; ca = and; ādyam = primal; puruṣam = person; prapadye = I take refuge; yataḥ = from whom; pravṛttiḥ = the activity; prasṛtā = has flowed forth; purāṇī = ancient
NOTE: "I take refuge" is the seeker's own thought, spoken in the first person. It can also be read as "one should take refuge".

15.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्गदोषा / अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः । / द्वन्द्वैर्विमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञै- / र्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पदमव्ययं तत् ॥
LIT: Without pride and delusion, with the fault of attachment conquered, constant in what concerns the self, with desires turned away, freed from the pairs named pleasure and pain, the undeluded go to that undecaying place.
WORDS: nirmāna-mohāḥ = without pride and delusion; jita-saṅga-doṣāḥ = with the fault of attachment conquered; adhyātma-nityāḥ = constant in what concerns the self; vinivṛtta-kāmāḥ = with desires turned away; dvandvaiḥ = from the pairs; vimuktāḥ = freed; sukha-duḥkha-saṁjñaiḥ = named pleasure and pain; gacchanti = they go; amūḍhāḥ = the undeluded; padam = to the place; avyayam = undecaying; tat = that

15.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न तद्भासयते सूर्यो न शशाङ्को न पावकः । / यद्गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥
LIT: The sun does not light up that, nor the moon, nor fire. That to which, having gone, they do not return is my supreme abode.
WORDS: na = not; tat = that; bhāsayate = lights up; sūryaḥ = the sun; na = not; śaśāṅkaḥ = the moon; na = not; pāvakaḥ = fire; yat = to which; gatvā = having gone; na = not; nivartante = they return; tat = that; dhāma = abode; paramam = supreme; mama = my

15.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः । / मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति ॥
LIT: A part of me indeed, eternal, having become a living being in the world of the living, draws the senses, with the mind as the sixth, resting in nature.
WORDS: mama = of me; eva = indeed; aṁśaḥ = a part; jīva-loke = in the world of the living; jīva-bhūtaḥ = having become a living being; sanātanaḥ = eternal; manaḥ-ṣaṣṭhāni = with the mind as the sixth; indriyāṇi = the senses; prakṛti-sthāni = resting in nature; karṣati = it draws

15.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: शरीरं यदवाप्नोति यच्चाप्युत्क्रामतीश्वरः । / गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्धानिवाशयात् ॥
LIT: When the lord obtains a body, and also when he departs, he goes having taken these, like the wind taking scents from their resting place.
WORDS: śarīram = a body; yat = when; avāpnoti = he obtains; yat = when; ca = and; api = also; utkrāmati = he departs; īśvaraḥ = the lord; gṛhītvā = having taken; etāni = these; saṁyāti = he goes; vāyuḥ = the wind; gandhān = scents; iva = like; āśayāt = from their resting place
NOTE: "When the lord obtains a body, and also when he departs" can also be read as "whatever body the lord obtains, and whatever body he departs from".

15.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च । / अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते ॥
LIT: Presiding over hearing, sight and touch, taste and indeed smell, and the mind, this one attends on the objects of sense.
WORDS: śrotram = hearing; cakṣuḥ = sight; sparśanam = touch; ca = and; rasanam = taste; ghrāṇam = smell; eva = indeed; ca = and; adhiṣṭhāya = presiding over; manaḥ = the mind; ca = and; ayam = this one; viṣayān = the objects of sense; upasevate = attends on

15.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम् । / विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः ॥
LIT: Departing, or also staying, or experiencing, joined with the guṇas: the deluded do not see him. Those with the eye of knowledge see.
WORDS: utkrāmantam = departing; sthitam = staying; vā = or; api = also; bhuñjānam = experiencing; vā = or; guṇa-anvitam = joined with the guṇas; vimūḍhāḥ = the deluded; na = not; anupaśyanti = they see; paśyanti = they see; jñāna-cakṣuṣaḥ = those with the eye of knowledge

15.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पश्यन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम् । / यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पश्यन्त्यचेतसः ॥
LIT: And the striving yogīs see this one established in the self. Even though striving, those whose self is unformed, the unthinking, do not see this one.
WORDS: yatantaḥ = striving; yoginaḥ = the yogīs; ca = and; enam = this one; paśyanti = they see; ātmani = in the self; avasthitam = established; yatantaḥ = striving; api = even though; akṛta-ātmānaḥ = those whose self is unformed; na = not; enam = this one; paśyanti = they see; acetasaḥ = the unthinking
NOTE: "Established in the self" can also be read as "established in themselves", meaning in their own body or mind.

15.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम् । / यच्चन्द्रमसि यच्चाग्नौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम् ॥
LIT: The brilliance residing in the sun which lights up the whole world, which is in the moon and which is in fire: know that brilliance to be mine.
WORDS: yat = which; āditya-gatam = residing in the sun; tejaḥ = the brilliance; jagat = the world; bhāsayate = lights up; akhilam = whole; yat = which; candramasi = in the moon; yat = which; ca = and; agnau = in fire; tat = that; tejaḥ = brilliance; viddhi = know; māmakam = mine

15.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: गामाविश्य च भूतानि धारयाम्यहमोजसा । / पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः ॥
LIT: And having entered the earth, I support beings by vigour, and I nourish all the plants, having become the moon, whose nature is sap.
WORDS: gām = the earth; āviśya = having entered; ca = and; bhūtāni = beings; dhārayāmi = I support; aham = I; ojasā = by vigour; puṣṇāmi = I nourish; ca = and; oṣadhīḥ = the plants; sarvāḥ = all; somaḥ = the moon; bhūtvā = having become; rasa-ātmakaḥ = whose nature is sap
NOTE: "The moon, whose nature is sap" can also be read as "the soma plant, whose nature is juice".

15.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः । / प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम् ॥
LIT: Having become the fire common to all men, dwelling in the body of breathing creatures, joined with the outgoing and incoming breaths, I cook food of four kinds.
WORDS: aham = I; vaiśvānaraḥ = the fire common to all men; bhūtvā = having become; prāṇinām = of breathing creatures; deham = the body; āśritaḥ = dwelling in; prāṇa-apāna-samāyuktaḥ = joined with the outgoing and incoming breaths; pacāmi = I cook; annam = food; catur-vidham = of four kinds
NOTE: "I cook" can also be read as "I digest".

15.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि सन्निविष्टो / मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनञ्च । / वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो / वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम् ॥
LIT: And I am seated in the heart of all. From me are memory, knowledge and their removal. And by all the Vedas I alone am to be known. I am the maker of the end of the Vedas and indeed the knower of the Vedas.
WORDS: sarvasya = of all; ca = and; aham = I; hṛdi = in the heart; sanniviṣṭaḥ = seated; mattaḥ = from me; smṛtiḥ = memory; jñānam = knowledge; apohanam = their removal; ca = and; vedaiḥ = by the Vedas; ca = and; sarvaiḥ = all; aham = I; eva = alone; vedyaḥ = to be known; vedānta-kṛt = the maker of the end of the Vedas; veda-vit = the knower of the Vedas; eva = indeed; ca = and; aham = I
NOTE: "Their removal" can also be read as "reasoning", the power of working through doubts.

15.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: द्वाविमौ पुरुषौ लोके क्षरश्चाक्षर एव च । / क्षरः सर्वाणि भूतानि कूटस्थोऽक्षर उच्यते ॥
LIT: There are these two persons in the world: the perishable and indeed the imperishable. The perishable is all beings. The one standing at the peak is called the imperishable.
WORDS: dvau = two; imau = these; puruṣau = persons; loke = in the world; kṣaraḥ = the perishable; ca = and; akṣaraḥ = the imperishable; eva = indeed; ca = and; kṣaraḥ = the perishable; sarvāṇi = all; bhūtāni = beings; kūṭa-sthaḥ = the one standing at the peak; akṣaraḥ = the imperishable; ucyate = is called
NOTE: "The one standing at the peak" can also be read as "the one who stands unchanging, like an anvil".

15.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: उत्तमः पुरुषस्त्वन्यः परमात्मेत्युदाहृतः । / यो लोकत्रयमाविश्य बिभर्त्यव्यय ईश्वरः ॥
LIT: But the highest person is another, declared thus as the supreme self, the undecaying lord who, having entered the three worlds, sustains them.
WORDS: uttamaḥ = the highest; puruṣaḥ = person; tu = but; anyaḥ = another; parama-ātmā = the supreme self; iti = thus; udāhṛtaḥ = declared; yaḥ = who; loka-trayam = the three worlds; āviśya = having entered; bibharti = sustains; avyayaḥ = undecaying; īśvaraḥ = the lord

15.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः । / अतोऽस्मि लोके वेदे च प्रथितः पुरुषोत्तमः ॥
LIT: Because I have gone beyond the perishable and am higher even than the imperishable, therefore I am celebrated in the world and in the Veda as the highest person.
WORDS: yasmāt = because; kṣaram = the perishable; atītaḥ = gone beyond; aham = I; akṣarāt = than the imperishable; api = even; ca = and; uttamaḥ = higher; ataḥ = therefore; asmi = I am; loke = in the world; vede = in the Veda; ca = and; prathitaḥ = celebrated; puruṣa-uttamaḥ = the highest person

15.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम् । / स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत ॥
LIT: He who, undeluded, thus knows me as the highest person, he, knowing all, worships me with his whole being, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: yaḥ = he who; mām = me; evam = thus; asammūḍhaḥ = undeluded; jānāti = knows; puruṣa-uttamam = as the highest person; saḥ = he; sarva-vit = knowing all; bhajati = worships; mām = me; sarva-bhāvena = with his whole being; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata
NOTE: "With his whole being" can also be read as "in every way".

15.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रमिदमुक्तं मयानघ । / एतद्बुद्ध्वा बुद्धिमान्स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत ॥
LIT: Thus this most secret teaching has been spoken by me, O sinless one. Having understood this, one would be possessed of buddhi and one who has done what is to be done, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: iti = thus; guhyatamam = most secret; śāstram = teaching; idam = this; uktam = has been spoken; mayā = by me; anagha = O sinless one; etat = this; buddhvā = having understood; buddhimān = possessed of buddhi; syāt = one would be; kṛta-kṛtyaḥ = one who has done what is to be done; ca = and; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata
