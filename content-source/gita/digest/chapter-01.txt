1.1 [Dhṛtarāṣṭra speaks]
SA: धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः । / मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय ॥
LIT: On the field of dharma, on the field of the Kurus, assembled and eager to fight, what did my own and the sons of Pāṇḍu as well do, O Sañjaya?
WORDS: dharma-kṣetre = on the field of dharma; kuru-kṣetre = on the field of the Kurus; samavetāḥ = assembled; yuyutsavaḥ = eager to fight; māmakāḥ = my own; pāṇḍavāḥ = the sons of Pāṇḍu; ca = and; eva = as well; kim = what; akurvata = they did; sañjaya = O Sañjaya

1.2 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा । / आचार्यमुपसङ्गम्य राजा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥
LIT: But having seen the army of the sons of Pāṇḍu arrayed, King Duryodhana then, having approached the teacher, spoke a word.
WORDS: dṛṣṭvā = having seen; tu = but; pāṇḍava-anīkam = the army of the sons of Pāṇḍu; vyūḍham = arrayed; duryodhanaḥ = Duryodhana; tadā = then; ācāryam = the teacher; upasaṅgamya = having approached; rājā = the king; vacanam = a word; abravīt = he spoke

1.3 [Sañjaya speaks]

NOTE: Verses 4 to 6 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

1.9 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: अन्ये च बहवः शूरा मदर्थे त्यक्तजीविताः । / नानाशस्त्रप्रहरणाः सर्वे युद्धविशारदाः ॥

1.10 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: अपर्याप्तं तदस्माकं बलं भीष्माभिरक्षितम् । / पर्याप्तं त्विदमेतेषां बलं भीमाभिरक्षितम् ॥
LIT: That force of ours, guarded by Bhīṣma, is insufficient. But this force of these, guarded by Bhīma, is sufficient.
WORDS: aparyāptam = insufficient; tat = that; asmākam = of ours; balam = force; bhīṣma-abhirakṣitam = guarded by Bhīṣma; paryāptam = sufficient; tu = but; idam = this; eteṣām = of these; balam = force; bhīma-abhirakṣitam = guarded by Bhīma
NOTE: "Insufficient" can also be read as "unlimited", and "sufficient" as "limited", which makes Duryodhana confident in his own army rather than anxious about it.

1.11 [Sañjaya speaks]
SA: अयनेषु च सर्वेषु यथाभागमवस्थिताः । / भीष्ममेवाभिरक्षन्तु भवन्तः सर्व एव हि ॥
