17.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः । / तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः ॥
LIT: Those who, having set aside the rule of scripture, sacrifice endowed with faith: what indeed is their standing, O Kṛṣṇa? Is it sattva, or rajas, or tamas?
WORDS: ye = those who; śāstra-vidhim = the rule of scripture; utsṛjya = having set aside; yajante = they sacrifice; śraddhayā = with faith; anvitāḥ = endowed; teṣām = of them; niṣṭhā = standing; tu = indeed; kā = what; kṛṣṇa = O Kṛṣṇa; sattvam = sattva; āho = or; rajaḥ = rajas; tamaḥ = tamas

17.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा । / सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु ॥
LIT: The faith of the embodied ones is of three kinds. It is born of their own nature: of sattva, and indeed of rajas, and of tamas. Hear of it thus.
WORDS: tri-vidhā = of three kinds; bhavati = is; śraddhā = faith; dehinām = of the embodied ones; sā = it; svabhāva-jā = born of their own nature; sāttvikī = of sattva; rājasī = of rajas; ca = and; eva = indeed; tāmasī = of tamas; ca = and; iti = thus; tām = of it; śṛṇu = hear

17.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत । / श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥
LIT: The faith of everyone is in keeping with his nature, O descendant of Bharata. This person is made of faith. Whatever faith one has, that indeed he is.
WORDS: sattva-anurūpā = in keeping with his nature; sarvasya = of everyone; śraddhā = faith; bhavati = is; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata; śraddhā-mayaḥ = made of faith; ayam = this; puruṣaḥ = person; yaḥ = who; yat-śraddhaḥ = having whatever faith; saḥ = that; eva = indeed; saḥ = he
NOTE: "In keeping with his nature" can also be read as "in keeping with his mind", or as "in keeping with the sattva in him".

17.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यजन्ते सात्त्विका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः । / प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जनाः ॥
LIT: Those of sattva worship the gods, those of rajas the yakṣas and rākṣasas. The others, the people of tamas, worship the departed and the hosts of spirits.
WORDS: yajante = they worship; sāttvikāḥ = those of sattva; devān = the gods; yakṣa-rakṣāṁsi = the yakṣas and rākṣasas; rājasāḥ = those of rajas; pretān = the departed; bhūta-gaṇān = the hosts of spirits; ca = and; anye = the others; yajante = they worship; tāmasāḥ = of tamas; janāḥ = people

17.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अशास्त्रविहितं घोरं तप्यन्ते ये तपो जनाः । / दम्भाहङ्कारसंयुक्ताः कामरागबलान्विताः ॥
LIT: The people who practise fierce austerity not ordained by scripture, joined to hypocrisy and ego, endowed with the force of desire and passion,
WORDS: aśāstra-vihitam = not ordained by scripture; ghoram = fierce; tapyante = they practise; ye = who; tapaḥ = austerity; janāḥ = people; dambha-ahaṅkāra-saṁyuktāḥ = joined to hypocrisy and ego; kāma-rāga-bala-anvitāḥ = endowed with the force of desire and passion
NOTE: Verses 5 to 6 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.
NOTE: "Endowed with the force of desire and passion" can also be read as "endowed with desire, passion and force".

17.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कर्षयन्तः शरीरस्थं भूतग्राममचेतसः । / मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्थं तान्विद्ध्यासुरनिश्चयान् ॥
LIT: senseless, tormenting the group of elements standing in the body, and also me standing within the body: know them to be of demonic resolve.
WORDS: karṣayantaḥ = tormenting; śarīra-stham = standing in the body; bhūta-grāmam = the group of elements; acetasaḥ = senseless; mām = me; ca = and; eva = also; antaḥ-śarīra-stham = standing within the body; tān = them; viddhi = know; āsura-niścayān = of demonic resolve

17.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः । / यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं शृणु ॥
LIT: But the food also that is dear to everyone is of three kinds, and likewise sacrifice, austerity and giving. Hear this distinction of them.
WORDS: āhāraḥ = food; tu = but; api = also; sarvasya = of everyone; tri-vidhaḥ = of three kinds; bhavati = is; priyaḥ = dear; yajñaḥ = sacrifice; tapaḥ = austerity; tathā = likewise; dānam = giving; teṣām = of them; bhedam = the distinction; imam = this; śṛṇu = hear

17.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आयुःसत्त्वबलारोग्यसुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः । / रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥
LIT: Foods that increase life, vitality, strength, health, happiness and delight, that are juicy, oily, firm and pleasing to the heart, are dear to one of sattva.
WORDS: āyuḥ-sattva-bala-ārogya-sukha-prīti-vivardhanāḥ = increasing life, vitality, strength, health, happiness and delight; rasyāḥ = juicy; snigdhāḥ = oily; sthirāḥ = firm; hṛdyāḥ = pleasing to the heart; āhārāḥ = foods; sāttvika-priyāḥ = dear to one of sattva
NOTE: "Firm" can also be read as "lasting", that is, food that sustains for a long time.

17.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कट्वम्ललवणात्युष्णतीक्ष्णरूक्षविदाहिनः । / आहारा राजसस्येष्टा दुःखशोकामयप्रदाः ॥
LIT: Foods that are pungent, sour, salty, very hot, sharp, dry and burning are wished for by one of rajas. They give pain, grief and sickness.
WORDS: kaṭu-amla-lavaṇa-ati-uṣṇa-tīkṣṇa-rūkṣa-vidāhinaḥ = pungent, sour, salty, very hot, sharp, dry and burning; āhārāḥ = foods; rājasasya = of one of rajas; iṣṭāḥ = wished for; duḥkha-śoka-āmaya-pradāḥ = giving pain, grief and sickness

17.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यातयामं गतरसं पूति पर्युषितं च यत् । / उच्छिष्टमपि चामेध्यं भोजनं तामसप्रियम् ॥
LIT: Food which is stale, with its taste gone, putrid and left overnight, and also left over by others and unfit for offering, is dear to one of tamas.
WORDS: yāta-yāmam = stale; gata-rasam = with its taste gone; pūti = putrid; paryuṣitam = left overnight; ca = and; yat = which; ucchiṣṭam = left over by others; api = also; ca = and; amedhyam = unfit for offering; bhojanam = food; tāmasa-priyam = dear to one of tamas

17.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते । / यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः ॥
LIT: The sacrifice seen in the rule, which is offered by those not longing for fruit, having settled the mind thus: 'it is simply to be offered', that is of sattva.
WORDS: aphala-ākāṅkṣibhiḥ = by those not longing for fruit; yajñaḥ = sacrifice; vidhi-dṛṣṭaḥ = seen in the rule; yaḥ = which; ijyate = is offered; yaṣṭavyam = it is to be offered; eva = simply; iti = thus; manaḥ = the mind; samādhāya = having settled; saḥ = that; sāttvikaḥ = of sattva

17.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अभिसन्धाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् । / इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम् ॥
LIT: But that which is offered having aimed at fruit, and indeed also for the sake of show, O best of the Bharatas, know that sacrifice to be of rajas.
WORDS: abhisandhāya = having aimed at; tu = but; phalam = fruit; dambha-artham = for the sake of show; api = also; ca = and; eva = indeed; yat = which; ijyate = is offered; bharata-śreṣṭha = O best of the Bharatas; tam = that; yajñam = sacrifice; viddhi = know; rājasam = of rajas

17.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् । / श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते ॥
LIT: A sacrifice lacking the rule, with no food given out, lacking the sacred words, without the gift to the priest, devoid of faith, they declare to be of tamas.
WORDS: vidhi-hīnam = lacking the rule; asṛṣṭa-annam = with no food given out; mantra-hīnam = lacking the sacred words; adakṣiṇam = without the gift to the priest; śraddhā-virahitam = devoid of faith; yajñam = sacrifice; tāmasam = of tamas; paricakṣate = they declare

17.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: देवद्विजगुरुप्राज्ञपूजनं शौचमार्जवम् । / ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते ॥
LIT: Honouring of the gods, the twice-born, the teachers and the wise, purity, straightness, celibacy and non-harming are called austerity of the body.
WORDS: deva-dvija-guru-prājña-pūjanam = honouring of the gods, the twice-born, the teachers and the wise; śaucam = purity; ārjavam = straightness; brahmacaryam = celibacy; ahiṁsā = non-harming; ca = and; śārīram = of the body; tapaḥ = austerity; ucyate = is called

17.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् । / स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते ॥
LIT: Speech which causes no agitation, which is true, pleasant and beneficial, and also the practice of one's own recitation, are called austerity made of speech.
WORDS: anudvega-karam = causing no agitation; vākyam = speech; satyam = true; priya-hitam = pleasant and beneficial; ca = and; yat = which; svādhyāya-abhyasanam = the practice of one's own recitation; ca = and; eva = also; vāk-mayam = made of speech; tapaḥ = austerity; ucyate = is called

17.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मनः प्रसादः सौम्यत्वं मौनमात्मविनिग्रहः । / भावसंशुद्धिरित्येतत्तपो मानसमुच्यते ॥
LIT: Calmness of mind, gentleness, silence, restraint of the self, purity of feeling: this is called austerity of the mind.
WORDS: manaḥ-prasādaḥ = calmness of mind; saumyatvam = gentleness; maunam = silence; ātma-vinigrahaḥ = restraint of the self; bhāva-saṁśuddhiḥ = purity of feeling; iti = thus; etat = this; tapaḥ = austerity; mānasam = of the mind; ucyate = is called

17.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: श्रद्धया परया तप्तं तपस्तत्त्रिविधं नरैः । / अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्युक्तैः सात्त्विकं परिचक्षते ॥
LIT: That threefold austerity, practised with supreme faith by men who are yoked and not longing for fruit, they declare to be of sattva.
WORDS: śraddhayā = with faith; parayā = supreme; taptam = practised; tapaḥ = austerity; tat = that; tri-vidham = threefold; naraiḥ = by men; aphala-ākāṅkṣibhiḥ = by those not longing for fruit; yuktaiḥ = by the yoked; sāttvikam = of sattva; paricakṣate = they declare

17.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सत्कारमानपूजार्थं तपो दम्भेन चैव यत् । / क्रियते तदिह प्रोक्तं राजसं चलमध्रुवम् ॥
LIT: The austerity which is done for the sake of good treatment, respect and honour, and indeed with hypocrisy, that is here said to be of rajas, moving and unlasting.
WORDS: satkāra-māna-pūjā-artham = for the sake of good treatment, respect and honour; tapaḥ = austerity; dambhena = with hypocrisy; ca = and; eva = indeed; yat = which; kriyate = is done; tat = that; iha = here; proktam = is said; rājasam = of rajas; calam = moving; adhruvam = unlasting

17.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मूढग्राहेणात्मनो यत्पीडया क्रियते तपः । / परस्योत्सादनार्थं वा तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥
LIT: The austerity which is done with a deluded grasp, with torment of the self, or for the sake of destroying another, that is declared to be of tamas.
WORDS: mūḍha-grāheṇa = with a deluded grasp; ātmanaḥ = of the self; yat = which; pīḍayā = with torment; kriyate = is done; tapaḥ = austerity; parasya = of another; utsādana-artham = for the sake of destroying; vā = or; tat = that; tāmasam = of tamas; udāhṛtam = is declared
NOTE: "With torment of the self" can also be read as "with torment of oneself", that is, of one's own body.

17.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे । / देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥
LIT: The gift which is given thus: 'it is to be given', to one who does no favour in return, in the proper place and at the proper time and to a worthy receiver, that gift is remembered as of sattva.
WORDS: dātavyam = it is to be given; iti = thus; yat = which; dānam = gift; dīyate = is given; anupakāriṇe = to one who does no favour in return; deśe = in the proper place; kāle = at the proper time; ca = and; pātre = to a worthy receiver; ca = and; tat = that; dānam = gift; sāttvikam = of sattva; smṛtam = is remembered
NOTE: "To one who does no favour in return" can also be read as "to one who has done no favour before".

17.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः । / दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥

WORDS: yat = which; tu = but; prati-upakāra-artham = for the sake of a favour in return; phalam = fruit; uddiśya = having aimed at; vā = or; punaḥ = again; dīyate = is given; ca = and; parikliṣṭam = grudgingly; tat = that; dānam = gift; rājasam = of rajas; smṛtam = is remembered

17.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अदेशकाले यद्दानमपात्रेभ्यश्च दीयते । / असत्कृतमवज्ञातं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥

17.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ॐतत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः । / ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥
LIT: 'Oṁ, tat, sat': thus the threefold designation of brahman is remembered. By it the brahmins and the Vedas and the sacrifices were ordained of old.
WORDS: oṁ = oṁ; tat = tat, "that"; sat = sat, "real, good"; iti = thus; nirdeśaḥ = the designation; brahmaṇaḥ = of brahman; tri-vidhaḥ = threefold; smṛtaḥ = is remembered; brāhmaṇāḥ = the brahmins; tena = by it; vedāḥ = the Vedas; ca = and; yajñāḥ = the sacrifices; ca = and; vihitāḥ = were ordained; purā = of old
NOTE: "The brahmins" can also be read as "the Brāhmaṇas", the ritual texts attached to the Vedas.

17.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तस्मादोमित्युदाहृत्य यज्ञदानतपःक्रियाः । / प्रवर्तन्ते विधानोक्ताः सततं ब्रह्मवादिनाम् ॥

NOTE: "Those who speak of brahman" can also be read as "those who recite the Veda".

17.25 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तदित्यनभिसन्धाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः । / दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥

17.26 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते । / प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सच्छब्दः पार्थ युज्यते ॥

17.27 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सदिति चोच्यते । / कर्म चैव तदर्थीयं सदित्येवाभिधीयते ॥

17.28 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अश्रद्धया हुतं दत्तं तपस्तप्तं कृतं च यत् । / असदित्युच्यते पार्थ न च तत्प्रेत्य नो इह ॥
