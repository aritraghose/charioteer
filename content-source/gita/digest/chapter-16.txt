16.1 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः । / दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम् ॥
LIT: Fearlessness, purity of being, steadfastness in the yoga of knowledge, giving and self-restraint and sacrifice, study of the scriptures, austerity, straightness,
WORDS: abhayam = fearlessness; sattva-saṁśuddhiḥ = purity of being; jñāna-yoga-vyavasthitiḥ = steadfastness in the yoga of knowledge; dānam = giving; damaḥ = self-restraint; ca = and; yajñaḥ = sacrifice; ca = and; svādhyāyaḥ = study of the scriptures; tapaḥ = austerity; ārjavam = straightness
NOTE: Verses 1 to 3 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.
NOTE: "Steadfastness in the yoga of knowledge" can also be read as "steadfastness in knowledge and in yoga", two things rather than one.

16.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोधस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैशुनम् । / दया भूतेष्वलोलुप्त्वं मार्दवं ह्रीरचापलम् ॥
LIT: non-harming, truth, freedom from anger, giving up, peace, not slandering, compassion towards beings, freedom from greed, gentleness, modesty, freedom from fickleness,
WORDS: ahiṁsā = non-harming; satyam = truth; akrodhaḥ = freedom from anger; tyāgaḥ = giving up; śāntiḥ = peace; apaiśunam = not slandering; dayā = compassion; bhūteṣu = towards beings; aloluptvam = freedom from greed; mārdavam = gentleness; hrīḥ = modesty; acāpalam = freedom from fickleness

16.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता । / भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत ॥
LIT: vigour, forbearance, firmness, cleanliness, freedom from malice, no excessive pride: they are of one born to the divine endowment, O descendant of Bharata.
WORDS: tejaḥ = vigour; kṣamā = forbearance; dhṛtiḥ = firmness; śaucam = cleanliness; adrohaḥ = freedom from malice; na = not; ati-mānitā = excessive pride; bhavanti = they are; sampadam = to the endowment; daivīm = divine; abhijātasya = of one born; bhārata = O descendant of Bharata

16.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: दम्भो दर्पोऽभिमानश्च क्रोधः पारुष्यमेव च । / अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ सम्पदमासुरीम् ॥
LIT: Hypocrisy, arrogance and conceit, anger and indeed harshness, and ignorance are of one born, O son of Pṛthā, to the demonic endowment.
WORDS: dambhaḥ = hypocrisy; darpaḥ = arrogance; abhimānaḥ = conceit; ca = and; krodhaḥ = anger; pāruṣyam = harshness; eva = indeed; ca = and; ajñānam = ignorance; ca = and; abhijātasya = of one born; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; sampadam = to the endowment; āsurīm = demonic

16.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता । / मा शुचः सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव ॥
LIT: The divine endowment is held to be for release, the demonic for bondage. Do not grieve. You are born to the divine endowment, O son of Pāṇḍu.
WORDS: daivī = divine; sampat = the endowment; vimokṣāya = for release; nibandhāya = for bondage; āsurī = the demonic; matā = is held; mā = do not; śucaḥ = grieve; sampadam = to the endowment; daivīm = divine; abhijātaḥ = born; asi = you are; pāṇḍava = O son of Pāṇḍu

16.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च । / दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु ॥
LIT: There are two creations of beings in this world, the divine and indeed the demonic. The divine has been told at length. Hear from me the demonic, O son of Pṛthā.
WORDS: dvau = two; bhūta-sargau = creations of beings; loke = in the world; asmin = in this; daivaḥ = the divine; āsuraḥ = the demonic; eva = indeed; ca = and; daivaḥ = the divine; vistaraśaḥ = at length; proktaḥ = has been told; āsuram = the demonic; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; me = from me; śṛṇu = hear

16.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः । / न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते ॥
LIT: Demonic people do not know engaging in action and ceasing from action. Cleanliness is not found in them, nor even right conduct, nor truth.
WORDS: pravṛttim = engaging in action; ca = and; nivṛttim = ceasing from action; ca = and; janāḥ = people; na = not; viduḥ = they know; āsurāḥ = demonic; na = not; śaucam = cleanliness; na = nor; api = even; ca = and; ācāraḥ = right conduct; na = nor; satyam = truth; teṣu = in them; vidyate = is found
NOTE: "Engaging in action and ceasing from action" can also be read as "what is to be done and what is to be left undone".

16.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम् । / अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम् ॥
LIT: They say the world is without truth, without foundation, without a lord, born of one with another, having desire as its cause. What else?
WORDS: asatyam = without truth; apratiṣṭham = without foundation; te = they; jagat = the world; āhuḥ = they say; anīśvaram = without a lord; aparaspara-sambhūtam = born of one with another; kim = what; anyat = else; kāma-haitukam = having desire as its cause
NOTE: "Born of one with another" can also be read as "not arising in any ordered sequence of causes", that is, coming about by chance.

16.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः । / प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः ॥
LIT: Holding to this view, those whose self is lost, of small buddhi, of fierce action, hostile, come forth for the destruction of the world.
WORDS: etām = this; dṛṣṭim = view; avaṣṭabhya = holding to; naṣṭa-ātmānaḥ = those whose self is lost; alpa-buddhayaḥ = of small buddhi; prabhavanti = they come forth; ugra-karmāṇaḥ = of fierce action; kṣayāya = for the destruction; jagataḥ = of the world; ahitāḥ = hostile

16.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः । / मोहाद्गृहीत्वासद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः ॥
LIT: Resorting to desire that is hard to fill, filled with hypocrisy, pride and intoxication, having seized false notions out of delusion, they act, being of impure vows.
WORDS: kāmam = desire; āśritya = resorting to; duṣpūram = hard to fill; dambha-māna-mada-anvitāḥ = filled with hypocrisy, pride and intoxication; mohāt = out of delusion; gṛhītvā = having seized; asat-grāhān = false notions; pravartante = they act; aśuci-vratāḥ = of impure vows

16.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: चिन्तामपरिमेयां च प्रलयान्तामुपाश्रिताः । / कामोपभोगपरमा एतावदिति निश्चिताः ॥
LIT: And given over to immeasurable anxiety ending at death, holding the enjoyment of desires as the highest, convinced that there is this much and no more,
WORDS: cintām = anxiety; aparimeyām = immeasurable; ca = and; pralaya-antām = ending at death; upāśritāḥ = given over to; kāma-upabhoga-paramāḥ = holding the enjoyment of desires as the highest; etāvat = this much; iti = thus; niścitāḥ = convinced
NOTE: Verses 11 to 12 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

16.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः । / ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसञ्चयान् ॥
LIT: bound by hundreds of ropes of hope, devoted to desire and anger, they strive, for the sake of enjoying desires, for hoards of wealth by unjust means.
WORDS: āśā-pāśa-śataiḥ = by hundreds of ropes of hope; baddhāḥ = bound; kāma-krodha-parāyaṇāḥ = devoted to desire and anger; īhante = they strive for; kāma-bhoga-artham = for the sake of enjoying desires; anyāyena = by unjust means; artha-sañcayān = hoards of wealth

16.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इदमद्य मया लब्धमिमं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् । / इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम् ॥
LIT: "This has been gained by me today, this wish I shall obtain. This there is, and this wealth also will be mine again,
WORDS: idam = this; adya = today; mayā = by me; labdham = has been gained; imam = this; prāpsye = I shall obtain; manaḥ-ratham = wish; idam = this; asti = there is; idam = this; api = also; me = mine; bhaviṣyati = will be; punaḥ = again; dhanam = wealth
NOTE: Verses 13 to 16 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

16.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: असौ मया हतः शत्रुर्हनिष्ये चापरानपि । / ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बलवान्सुखी ॥
LIT: that enemy has been slain by me, and I shall slay others too. I am the lord, I am the enjoyer, I am successful, strong, happy,
WORDS: asau = that; mayā = by me; hataḥ = has been slain; śatruḥ = enemy; haniṣye = I shall slay; ca = and; aparān = others; api = too; īśvaraḥ = the lord; aham = I; aham = I; bhogī = the enjoyer; siddhaḥ = successful; aham = I; balavān = strong; sukhī = happy

16.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया । / यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः ॥
LIT: I am wealthy, of high birth. Who else is there equal to me? I shall sacrifice, I shall give, I shall rejoice." Thus deluded by ignorance,
WORDS: āḍhyaḥ = wealthy; abhijanavān = of high birth; asmi = I am; kaḥ = who; anyaḥ = else; asti = is there; sadṛśaḥ = equal; mayā = to me; yakṣye = I shall sacrifice; dāsyāmi = I shall give; modiṣye = I shall rejoice; iti = thus; ajñāna-vimohitāḥ = deluded by ignorance

16.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अनेकचित्तविभ्रान्ता मोहजालसमावृताः । / प्रसक्ताः कामभोगेषु पतन्ति नरकेऽशुचौ ॥
LIT: led astray by many thoughts, covered by a net of delusion, attached to the enjoyments of desire, they fall into an impure hell.
WORDS: aneka-citta-vibhrāntāḥ = led astray by many thoughts; moha-jāla-samāvṛtāḥ = covered by a net of delusion; prasaktāḥ = attached; kāma-bhogeṣu = to the enjoyments of desire; patanti = they fall; narake = into hell; aśucau = impure

16.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आत्मसम्भाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः । / यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम् ॥
LIT: Holding the self in high esteem, stubborn, filled with the pride and intoxication of wealth, they sacrifice with sacrifices in name only, with hypocrisy, not according to rule,
WORDS: ātma-sambhāvitāḥ = holding the self in high esteem; stabdhāḥ = stubborn; dhana-māna-mada-anvitāḥ = filled with the pride and intoxication of wealth; yajante = they sacrifice; nāma-yajñaiḥ = with sacrifices in name only; te = they; dambhena = with hypocrisy; avidhi-pūrvakam = not according to rule
NOTE: Verses 17 to 18 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.
NOTE: "Filled with the pride and intoxication of wealth" can also be read as "filled with wealth, pride and intoxication", three separate things.

16.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः । / मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः ॥
LIT: resorting to egoism, force, arrogance, desire and anger, hating me in their own bodies and those of others, envious.
WORDS: ahaṅkāram = egoism; balam = force; darpam = arrogance; kāmam = desire; krodham = anger; ca = and; saṁśritāḥ = resorting to; mām = me; ātma-para-deheṣu = in their own bodies and those of others; pradviṣantaḥ = hating; abhyasūyakāḥ = envious

16.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् । / क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु ॥
LIT: Those hating ones, cruel, the lowest of men, inauspicious, I hurl ceaselessly in the cycles of birth into demonic wombs only.
WORDS: tān = those; aham = I; dviṣataḥ = hating ones; krūrān = cruel; saṁsāreṣu = in the cycles of birth; nara-adhamān = the lowest of men; kṣipāmi = I hurl; ajasram = ceaselessly; aśubhān = inauspicious; āsurīṣu = into demonic; eva = only; yoniṣu = into wombs

16.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आसुरीं योनिमापन्ना मूढा जन्मनि जन्मनि । / मामप्राप्यैव कौन्तेय ततो यान्त्यधमां गतिम् ॥
LIT: Having fallen into a demonic womb, the deluded, in birth after birth, not reaching me at all, O son of Kuntī, go from there to the lowest state.
WORDS: āsurīm = demonic; yonim = a womb; āpannāḥ = having fallen into; mūḍhāḥ = the deluded; janmani = in birth; janmani = after birth; mām = me; aprāpya = not reaching; eva = at all; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; tataḥ = from there; yānti = they go; adhamām = to the lowest; gatim = state

16.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः । / कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत् ॥
LIT: This is the threefold gate of hell, destroying of the self: desire, anger, likewise greed. Therefore one should give up this set of three.
WORDS: tri-vidham = threefold; narakasya = of hell; idam = this; dvāram = gate; nāśanam = destroying; ātmanaḥ = of the self; kāmaḥ = desire; krodhaḥ = anger; tathā = likewise; lobhaḥ = greed; tasmāt = therefore; etat = this; trayam = set of three; tyajet = one should give up

16.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः । / आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥
LIT: Freed from these three gates of darkness, O son of Kuntī, a man practises what is best for the self. From that he goes to the highest state.
WORDS: etaiḥ = from these; vimuktaḥ = freed; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; tamaḥ-dvāraiḥ = from the gates of darkness; tribhiḥ = from the three; naraḥ = a man; ācarati = practises; ātmanaḥ = for the self; śreyaḥ = what is best; tataḥ = from that; yāti = he goes; parām = to the highest; gatim = state
NOTE: "Gates of darkness" can also be read as "gates of tamas", naming the guṇa of that name rather than darkness in general.

16.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः । / न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम् ॥
LIT: He who, casting aside the rule of scripture, acts at the urging of desire, he does not attain success, nor happiness, nor the highest state.
WORDS: yaḥ = he who; śāstra-vidhim = the rule of scripture; utsṛjya = casting aside; vartate = acts; kāma-kārataḥ = at the urging of desire; na = not; saḥ = he; siddhim = success; avāpnoti = attains; na = nor; sukham = happiness; na = nor; parām = the highest; gatim = state

16.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ । / ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि ॥

WORDS: tasmāt = therefore; śāstram = scripture; pramāṇam = the measure; te = for you; kārya-akārya-vyavasthitau = in settling what is to be done and what is not to be done; jñātvā = having known; śāstra-vidhāna-uktam = declared by the ordinance of scripture; karma = action; kartum = to do; iha = here; arhasi = you ought
