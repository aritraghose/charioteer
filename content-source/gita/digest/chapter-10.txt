10.1 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: भूय एव महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः । / यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥
LIT: Once again indeed, O mighty-armed one, hear my supreme word, which I will speak to you, who are delighted, out of desire for your good.
WORDS: bhūyaḥ = once again; eva = indeed; mahā-bāho = O mighty-armed one; śṛṇu = hear; me = my; paramam = supreme; vacaḥ = word; yat = which; te = to you; aham = I; prīyamāṇāya = to one who is delighted; vakṣyāmi = I will speak; hita-kāmyayā = out of desire for your good

10.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः । / अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥
LIT: The hosts of gods do not know my origin, nor do the great seers. For I am the beginning of the gods and of the great seers in every way.
WORDS: na = not; me = my; viduḥ = they know; sura-gaṇāḥ = the hosts of gods; prabhavam = origin; na = nor; mahā-ṛṣayaḥ = the great seers; aham = I; ādiḥ = the beginning; hi = for; devānām = of the gods; mahā-ṛṣīṇām = of the great seers; ca = and; sarvaśaḥ = in every way

10.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् । / असम्मूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥
LIT: Whoever knows me as unborn and without beginning, the great lord of the worlds, he, undeluded among mortals, is freed from all evils.
WORDS: yaḥ = whoever; mām = me; ajam = unborn; anādim = without beginning; ca = and; vetti = knows; loka-mahā-īśvaram = the great lord of the worlds; asammūḍhaḥ = undeluded; saḥ = he; martyeṣu = among mortals; sarva-pāpaiḥ = from all evils; pramucyate = is freed

10.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसम्मोहः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः । / सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च ॥
LIT: Buddhi, knowledge, freedom from delusion, forbearance, truth, self-restraint, calm, pleasure, pain, coming to be, ceasing to be, fear and indeed fearlessness,
WORDS: buddhiḥ = buddhi; jñānam = knowledge; asammohaḥ = freedom from delusion; kṣamā = forbearance; satyam = truth; damaḥ = self-restraint; śamaḥ = calm; sukham = pleasure; duḥkham = pain; bhavaḥ = coming to be; abhāvaḥ = ceasing to be; bhayam = fear; ca = and; abhayam = fearlessness; eva = indeed; ca = and
NOTE: Verses 4 to 5 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

10.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः । / भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः ॥
LIT: non-harm, evenness, contentment, austerity, giving, fame and ill fame: the states of beings, of different kinds, arise from me alone.
WORDS: ahiṁsā = non-harm; samatā = evenness; tuṣṭiḥ = contentment; tapaḥ = austerity; dānam = giving; yaśaḥ = fame; ayaśaḥ = ill fame; bhavanti = they arise; bhāvāḥ = the states; bhūtānām = of beings; mattaḥ = from me; eva = alone; pṛthak-vidhāḥ = of different kinds

10.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तथा । / मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः ॥
LIT: The seven great seers, the four ancient ones, and likewise the Manus, having my nature, were born of the mind. Of them are these creatures in the world.
WORDS: mahā-ṛṣayaḥ = the great seers; sapta = seven; pūrve = the ancient ones; catvāraḥ = four; manavaḥ = the Manus; tathā = likewise; mat-bhāvāḥ = having my nature; mānasāḥ = of the mind; jātāḥ = born; yeṣām = of whom; loke = in the world; imāḥ = these; prajāḥ = creatures
NOTE: "The four ancient ones, and likewise the Manus" can also be read as "and likewise the four Manus of old", or with "ancient" describing the seven great seers.
NOTE: "Having my nature" can also be read as "having their being from me".

10.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः । / सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः ॥

10.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते । / इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः ॥
LIT: I am the origin of all. From me all proceeds. Having thought thus, the wise, endowed with feeling, worship me.

10.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम् । / कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च ॥
LIT: With thought on me, with life-breath gone to me, awakening one another and always telling of me, they are content and they delight.

10.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तेषां सततयुक्तानां भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम् । / ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते ॥
LIT: To them, constantly joined, worshipping with love, I give that buddhi-yoga by which they come to me.
WORDS: teṣām = to them; satata-yuktānām = to the constantly joined; bhajatām = to those worshipping; prīti-pūrvakam = with love; dadāmi = I give; buddhi-yogam = buddhi-yoga; tam = that; yena = by which; mām = to me; upayānti = they come; te = they
NOTE: "Worshipping with love, I give" can also be read as "worshipping, I give with love".

10.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं तमः । / नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो ज्ञानदीपेन भास्वता ॥
LIT: Out of compassion for them alone, I, abiding in the state of the self, destroy the darkness born of ignorance with the shining lamp of knowledge.
WORDS: teṣām = for them; eva = alone; anukampā-artham = out of compassion; aham = I; ajñāna-jam = born of ignorance; tamaḥ = the darkness; nāśayāmi = I destroy; ātma-bhāva-sthaḥ = abiding in the state of the self; jñāna-dīpena = with the lamp of knowledge; bhāsvatā = with the shining
NOTE: "Abiding in the state of the self" can also be read as "dwelling within their own being".

10.12 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् । / पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विभुम् ॥
LIT: You are the supreme brahman, the supreme abode, the supreme purifier. The eternal divine person, the first god, unborn and all-pervading,
WORDS: param = the supreme; brahma = brahman; param = the supreme; dhāma = abode; pavitram = purifier; paramam = the supreme; bhavān = you; puruṣam = the person; śāśvatam = eternal; divyam = divine; ādi-devam = the first god; ajam = unborn; vibhum = all-pervading
NOTE: Verses 12 to 13 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.
NOTE: "The supreme abode" can also be read as "the supreme light".

10.13 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: आहुस्त्वामृषयः सर्वे देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा । / असितो देवलो व्यासः स्वयं चैव ब्रवीषि मे ॥
LIT: all the seers call you that, and likewise the divine seer Nārada, Asita, Devala and Vyāsa, and indeed you yourself tell me.
WORDS: āhuḥ = they call; tvām = you; ṛṣayaḥ = the seers; sarve = all; deva-ṛṣiḥ = the divine seer; nāradaḥ = Nārada; tathā = likewise; asitaḥ = Asita; devalaḥ = Devala; vyāsaḥ = Vyāsa; svayam = yourself; ca = and; eva = indeed; bravīṣi = you tell; me = to me
NOTE: "Asita, Devala" can also be read as one seer, "Asita Devala".

10.14 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: सर्वमेतदृतं मन्ये यन्मां वदसि केशव । / न हि ते भगवन्व्यक्तिं विदुर्देवा न दानवाः ॥
LIT: All this which you say to me I think true, O Keśava. For neither the gods nor the Dānavas know your manifestation, O blessed lord.
WORDS: sarvam = all; etat = this; ṛtam = true; manye = I think; yat = which; mām = to me; vadasi = you say; keśava = O Keśava; na = not; hi = for; te = your; bhagavan = O blessed lord; vyaktim = manifestation; viduḥ = they know; devāḥ = the gods; na = nor; dānavāḥ = the Dānavas

10.15 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: स्वयमेवात्मनात्मानं वेत्थ त्वं पुरुषोत्तम । / भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते ॥
LIT: You yourself alone know the self by the self, O highest of persons, O bringer forth of beings, O lord of beings, O god of gods, O master of the world.
WORDS: svayam = yourself; eva = alone; ātmanā = by the self; ātmānam = the self; vettha = you know; tvam = you; puruṣa-uttama = O highest of persons; bhūta-bhāvana = O bringer forth of beings; bhūta-īśa = O lord of beings; deva-deva = O god of gods; jagat-pate = O master of the world

10.16 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषेण दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः । / याभिर्विभूतिभिर्लोकानिमांस्त्वं व्याप्य तिष्ठसि ॥
LIT: You ought to tell without remainder, for divine are the manifestations of the self, the manifestations by which you stand pervading these worlds.

10.17 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन् । / केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया ॥

WORDS: katham = how; vidyām = may I know; aham = I; yogin = O yogī; tvām = you; sadā = always; paricintayan = thinking of; keṣu = in which; keṣu = in which; ca = and; bhāveṣu = in states; cintyaḥ = to be thought of; asi = you are; bhagavan = O blessed lord; mayā = by me

10.18 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन । / भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि शृण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम् ॥

10.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः । / प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे ॥

SA: अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः । / अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च ॥

10.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: आदित्यानामहं विष्णुर्ज्योतिषां रविरंशुमान् । / मरीचिर्मरुतामस्मि नक्षत्राणामहं शशी ॥

10.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

10.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

10.30 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रह्लादश्चास्मि दैत्यानां कालः कलयतामहम् । / मृगाणां च मृगेन्द्रोऽहं वैनतेयश्च पक्षिणाम् ॥
LIT: And of the daityas I am Prahlāda. Of those who reckon I am time. And of wild animals I am the lord of animals, and of birds the son of Vinatā.
WORDS: prahlādaḥ = Prahlāda (the devout demon prince); ca = and; asmi = I am; daityānām = of the daityas (demons, sons of Diti); kālaḥ = time; kalayatām = of those who reckon; aham = I; mṛgāṇām = of wild animals; ca = and; mṛga-indraḥ = the lord of animals (the lion); aham = I; vainateyaḥ = the son of Vinatā (Garuḍa); ca = and; pakṣiṇām = of birds
NOTE: "Of those who reckon" can also be read as "of those who drive things on".

10.31 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: पवनः पवतामस्मि रामः शस्त्रभृतामहम् । / झषाणां मकरश्चास्मि स्रोतसामस्मि जाह्नवी ॥
LIT: Of purifiers I am the wind. Of those who bear weapons I am Rāma. And of fish I am the makara. Of streams I am the daughter of Jahnu.
WORDS: pavanaḥ = the wind; pavatām = of purifiers; asmi = I am; rāmaḥ = Rāma; śastra-bhṛtām = of those who bear weapons; aham = I; jhaṣāṇām = of fish; makaraḥ = the makara (a sea monster); ca = and; asmi = I am; srotasām = of streams; asmi = I am; jāhnavī = the daughter of Jahnu (the Ganges)
NOTE: "Of purifiers" can also be read as "of things that move swiftly".
NOTE: "Rāma" is usually taken to be Rāma the son of Daśaratha, but it can also be read as Rāma with the axe (Paraśurāma).
NOTE: "The makara" is a sea creature that is read variously as a shark or a crocodile.

10.32 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च मध्यं चैवाहमर्जुन । / अध्यात्मविद्या विद्यानां वादः प्रवदतामहम् ॥
LIT: Of creations I am the beginning and the end, and indeed the middle, O Arjuna. Of kinds of knowledge I am the knowledge of the self. Of those who debate I am the argument.
WORDS: sargāṇām = of creations; ādiḥ = the beginning; antaḥ = the end; ca = and; madhyam = the middle; ca = and; eva = indeed; aham = I; arjuna = O Arjuna; adhyātma-vidyā = the knowledge of the self; vidyānām = of kinds of knowledge; vādaḥ = the argument; pravadatām = of those who debate; aham = I
NOTE: "The argument" can also be read more narrowly as "honest debate that seeks the truth", as against debate carried on only to win.

10.33 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अक्षराणामकारोऽस्मि द्वन्द्वः सामासिकस्य च । / अहमेवाक्षयः कालो धाताहं विश्वतोमुखः ॥

10.34 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मृत्युः सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च भविष्यताम् । / कीर्तिः श्रीर्वाक्च नारीणां स्मृतिर्मेधा धृतिः क्षमा ॥

WORDS: mṛtyuḥ = death; sarva-haraḥ = that carries off all; ca = and; aham = I; udbhavaḥ = the origin; ca = and; bhaviṣyatām = of things that are to be; kīrtiḥ = fame; śrīḥ = fortune; vāk = speech; ca = and; nārīṇām = of feminine things; smṛtiḥ = memory; medhā = intelligence; dhṛtiḥ = steadfastness; kṣamā = forbearance
NOTE: "Of feminine things" can also be read as "among women". The seven qualities named are all feminine words in Sanskrit, and are also thought of as goddesses.

10.35 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् । / मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः ॥

10.36 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: द्यूतं छलयतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् । / जयोऽस्मि व्यवसायोऽस्मि सत्त्वं सत्त्ववतामहम् ॥

10.37 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: वृष्णीनां वासुदेवोऽस्मि पाण्डवानां धनञ्जयः । / मुनीनामप्यहं व्यासः कवीनामुशना कविः ॥

NOTE: "Of poets the poet Uśanas" can also be read as "of seers the seer Uśanas". The same word means both a poet and a wise seer.

10.38 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

10.39 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
