18.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: संन्यासस्य महाबाहो तत्त्वमिच्छामि वेदितुम् । / त्यागस्य च हृषीकेश पृथक्केशिनिषूदन ॥
LIT: O mighty-armed one, I wish to know the truth of renunciation, and of giving up, O lord of the senses, separately, O slayer of Keśī.
WORDS: saṁnyāsasya = of renunciation; mahā-bāho = O mighty-armed one; tattvam = the truth; icchāmi = I wish; veditum = to know; tyāgasya = of giving up; ca = and; hṛṣīkeśa = O lord of the senses; pṛthak = separately; keśi-niṣūdana = O slayer of Keśī

18.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासं संन्यासं कवयो विदुः । / सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं प्राहुस्त्यागं विचक्षणाः ॥
LIT: The poets know the laying down of actions done from desire as renunciation. The clear-sighted call the giving up of the fruit of all actions giving up.
WORDS: kāmyānām = of those done from desire; karmaṇām = of actions; nyāsam = the laying down; saṁnyāsam = renunciation; kavayaḥ = the poets; viduḥ = they know; sarva-karma-phala-tyāgam = the giving up of the fruit of all actions; prāhuḥ = they call; tyāgam = giving up; vicakṣaṇāḥ = the clear-sighted

18.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: त्याज्यं दोषवदित्येके कर्म प्राहुर्मनीषिणः । / यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यमिति चापरे ॥
LIT: Some thoughtful ones say: "Action, having fault, is to be given up." And others: "The action of sacrifice, giving and austerity is not to be given up."
WORDS: tyājyam = to be given up; doṣa-vat = having fault; iti = thus; eke = some; karma = action; prāhuḥ = they say; manīṣiṇaḥ = thoughtful ones; yajña-dāna-tapaḥ-karma = the action of sacrifice, giving and austerity; na = not; tyājyam = to be given up; iti = thus; ca = and; apare = others
NOTE: "Action, having fault, is to be given up" can also be read as "action is to be given up like a fault".

18.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: निश्चयं शृणु मे तत्र त्यागे भरतसत्तम । / त्यागो हि पुरुषव्याघ्र त्रिविधः सम्प्रकीर्तितः ॥
LIT: Hear my settled conclusion there, about giving up, O best of the Bharatas. For giving up, O tiger among men, is declared to be of three kinds.
WORDS: niścayam = the settled conclusion; śṛṇu = hear; me = my; tatra = there; tyāge = about giving up; bharata-sattama = O best of the Bharatas; tyāgaḥ = giving up; hi = for; puruṣa-vyāghra = O tiger among men; tri-vidhaḥ = of three kinds; samprakīrtitaḥ = is declared

18.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यं कार्यमेव तत् । / यज्ञो दानं तपश्चैव पावनानि मनीषिणाम् ॥
LIT: The action of sacrifice, giving and austerity is not to be given up. That is indeed to be done. Sacrifice, giving and austerity are indeed purifiers of the thoughtful.
WORDS: yajña-dāna-tapaḥ-karma = the action of sacrifice, giving and austerity; na = not; tyājyam = to be given up; kāryam = to be done; eva = indeed; tat = that; yajñaḥ = sacrifice; dānam = giving; tapaḥ = austerity; ca = and; eva = indeed; pāvanāni = purifiers; manīṣiṇām = of the thoughtful

18.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च । / कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम् ॥
LIT: But even these actions are to be done having given up attachment and the fruits. This, O son of Pṛthā, is my settled, highest view.
WORDS: etāni = these; api = even; tu = but; karmāṇi = actions; saṅgam = attachment; tyaktvā = having given up; phalāni = the fruits; ca = and; kartavyāni = to be done; iti = thus; me = my; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; niścitam = settled; matam = view; uttamam = highest

18.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नियतस्य तु संन्यासः कर्मणो नोपपद्यते । / मोहात्तस्य परित्यागस्तामसः परिकीर्तितः ॥
LIT: But renunciation of prescribed action is not fitting. The complete giving up of it from delusion is declared to be of tamas.
WORDS: niyatasya = of prescribed; tu = but; saṁnyāsaḥ = renunciation; karmaṇaḥ = of action; na = not; upapadyate = is fitting; mohāt = from delusion; tasya = of it; parityāgaḥ = the complete giving up; tāmasaḥ = of tamas; parikīrtitaḥ = is declared
NOTE: "Prescribed action" can also be read as "necessary action", meaning what one's own nature and place require.

18.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: दुःखमित्येव यत्कर्म कायक्लेशभयात्त्यजेत् । / स कृत्वा राजसं त्यागं नैव त्यागफलं लभेत् ॥
LIT: Whatever action one would give up merely as "painful", from fear of trouble to the body: he, having done a giving up of rajas, would not at all obtain the fruit of giving up.
WORDS: duḥkham = painful; iti = thus; eva = merely; yat = whatever; karma = action; kāya-kleśa-bhayāt = from fear of trouble to the body; tyajet = one would give up; saḥ = he; kṛtvā = having done; rājasam = of rajas; tyāgam = a giving up; na = not; eva = at all; tyāga-phalam = the fruit of giving up; labhet = would obtain

18.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कार्यमित्येव यत्कर्म नियतं क्रियतेऽर्जुन । / सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलं चैव स त्यागः सात्त्विको मतः ॥
LIT: Whatever prescribed action is done merely as "to be done", O Arjuna, having given up attachment and also the fruit: that giving up is considered to be of sattva.
WORDS: kāryam = to be done; iti = thus; eva = merely; yat = whatever; karma = action; niyatam = prescribed; kriyate = is done; arjuna = O Arjuna; saṅgam = attachment; tyaktvā = having given up; phalam = the fruit; ca = and; eva = also; saḥ = that; tyāgaḥ = giving up; sāttvikaḥ = of sattva; mataḥ = is considered

18.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न द्वेष्ट्यकुशलं कर्म कुशले नानुषज्जते । / त्यागी सत्त्वसमाविष्टो मेधावी छिन्नसंशयः ॥
LIT: The one who gives up, filled with sattva, intelligent, whose doubt is cut off, does not hate disagreeable action and does not cling to the agreeable.
WORDS: na = not; dveṣṭi = he hates; akuśalam = disagreeable; karma = action; kuśale = to the agreeable; na = not; anuṣajjate = he clings; tyāgī = the one who gives up; sattva-samāviṣṭaḥ = filled with sattva; medhāvī = intelligent; chinna-saṁśayaḥ = whose doubt is cut off
NOTE: "Disagreeable action" and "the agreeable" can also be read as "unskilled action" and "the skilled".

18.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न हि देहभृता शक्यं त्यक्तुं कर्माण्यशेषतः । / यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते ॥
LIT: For it is not possible for one who bears a body to give up actions without remainder. But he who gives up the fruit of action is called one who gives up.
WORDS: na = not; hi = for; deha-bhṛtā = by one who bears a body; śakyam = possible; tyaktum = to give up; karmāṇi = actions; aśeṣataḥ = without remainder; yaḥ = who; tu = but; karma-phala-tyāgī = one who gives up the fruit of action; saḥ = he; tyāgī = one who gives up; iti = thus; abhidhīyate = is called

18.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अनिष्टमिष्टं मिश्रं च त्रिविधं कर्मणः फलम् । / भवत्यत्यागिनां प्रेत्य न तु संन्यासिनां क्वचित् ॥
LIT: Unwanted, wanted and mixed: the fruit of action is of three kinds. It comes to be for those who do not give up, after departing, but not ever for renouncers.
WORDS: aniṣṭam = unwanted; iṣṭam = wanted; miśram = mixed; ca = and; tri-vidham = of three kinds; karmaṇaḥ = of action; phalam = the fruit; bhavati = it comes to be; atyāginām = for those who do not give up; pretya = after departing; na = not; tu = but; saṁnyāsinām = for renouncers; kvacit = ever

18.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: पञ्चैतानि महाबाहो कारणानि निबोध मे । / साङ्ख्ये कृतान्ते प्रोक्तानि सिद्धये सर्वकर्मणाम् ॥
LIT: Learn from me, O mighty-armed one, these five causes for the accomplishment of all actions, declared in the sāṅkhya, the settled conclusion.
WORDS: pañca = five; etāni = these; mahā-bāho = O mighty-armed one; kāraṇāni = causes; nibodha = learn; me = from me; sāṅkhye = in the sāṅkhya; kṛta-ante = in the settled conclusion; proktāni = declared; siddhaye = for the accomplishment; sarva-karmaṇām = of all actions
NOTE: "The settled conclusion" can also be read as "where action comes to its end".

18.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अधिष्ठानं तथा कर्ता करणं च पृथग्विधम् । / विविधाश्च पृथक्चेष्टा दैवं चैवात्र पञ्चमम् ॥
LIT: The seat, likewise the doer, and the instrument of separate kinds, and the various separate movements, and the divine is indeed the fifth here.
WORDS: adhiṣṭhānam = the seat; tathā = likewise; kartā = the doer; karaṇam = the instrument; ca = and; pṛthak-vidham = of separate kinds; vividhāḥ = various; ca = and; pṛthak = separate; ceṣṭāḥ = movements; daivam = the divine; ca = and; eva = indeed; atra = here; pañcamam = the fifth
NOTE: "The divine" can also be read as "fate".

18.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: शरीरवाङ्मनोभिर्यत्कर्म प्रारभते नरः । / न्याय्यं वा विपरीतं वा पञ्चैते तस्य हेतवः ॥
LIT: Whatever action a man undertakes with body, speech and mind, right or the opposite, these five are the causes of it.
WORDS: śarīra-vāk-manobhiḥ = with body, speech and mind; yat = whatever; karma = action; prārabhate = undertakes; naraḥ = a man; nyāyyam = right; vā = or; viparītam = the opposite; vā = or; pañca = five; ete = these; tasya = of it; hetavaḥ = the causes

18.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तत्रैवं सति कर्तारमात्मानं केवलं तु यः । / पश्यत्यकृतबुद्धित्वान्न स पश्यति दुर्मतिः ॥
LIT: But it being thus there, he who, from having an unformed buddhi, sees the self alone as the doer, he, of poor understanding, does not see.

NOTE: "The self alone as the doer" can also be read as "himself alone as the doer".

18.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यस्य नाहङ्कृतो भावो बुद्धिर्यस्य न लिप्यते । / हत्वाऽपि स इमाँल्लोकान्न हन्ति न निबध्यते ॥
LIT: He whose state of being is not made of the sense of I, whose buddhi is not stained, even having killed these worlds, does not kill and is not bound.

NOTE: "These worlds" can also be read as "these people".

18.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं परिज्ञाता त्रिविधा कर्मचोदना । / करणं कर्म कर्तेति त्रिविधः कर्मसङ्ग्रहः ॥
LIT: Knowledge, what is to be known and the knower: the urging to action is of three kinds. The instrument, the action and the doer: thus the sum of action is of three kinds.
WORDS: jñānam = knowledge; jñeyam = what is to be known; parijñātā = the knower; tri-vidhā = of three kinds; karma-codanā = the urging to action; karaṇam = the instrument; karma = the action; kartā = the doer; iti = thus; tri-vidhaḥ = of three kinds; karma-saṅgrahaḥ = the sum of action

18.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ज्ञानं कर्म च कर्ता च त्रिधैव गुणभेदतः । / प्रोच्यते गुणसङ्ख्याने यथावच्छृणु तान्यपि ॥
LIT: Knowledge, action and the doer are declared in the reckoning of the guṇas in only three ways, by the division of the guṇas. Hear those also, as they are.
WORDS: jñānam = knowledge; karma = action; ca = and; kartā = the doer; ca = and; tridhā = in three ways; eva = only; guṇa-bhedataḥ = by the division of the guṇas; procyate = is declared; guṇa-saṅkhyāne = in the reckoning of the guṇas; yathāvat = as they are; śṛṇu = hear; tāni = those; api = also

18.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्वभूतेषु येनैकं भावमव्ययमीक्षते । / अविभक्तं विभक्तेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि सात्त्विकम् ॥

18.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: पृथक्त्वेन तु यज्ज्ञानं नानाभावान्पृथग्विधान् । / वेत्ति सर्वेषु भूतेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि राजसम् ॥

18.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यत्तु कृत्स्नवदेकस्मिन्कार्ये सक्तमहैतुकम् । / अतत्त्वार्थवदल्पं च तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥

18.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नियतं सङ्गरहितमरागद्वेषतः कृतम् । / अफलप्रेप्सुना कर्म यत्तत्सात्त्विकमुच्यते ॥

18.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यत्तु कामेप्सुना कर्म साहङ्कारेण वा पुनः । / क्रियते बहुलायासं तद्राजसमुदाहृतम् ॥

18.25 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

18.26 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]

18.30 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
