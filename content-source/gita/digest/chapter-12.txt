12.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते । / ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥
LIT: The devotees who, thus constantly yoked, worship you, and also those who worship the imperishable, the unmanifest: of them, which are the best knowers of yoga?
WORDS: evam = thus; satata-yuktāḥ = constantly yoked; ye = those who; bhaktāḥ = devotees; tvām = you; paryupāsate = they worship; ye = those who; ca = and; api = also; akṣaram = the imperishable; avyaktam = the unmanifest; teṣām = of them; ke = which ones; yoga-vit-tamāḥ = the best knowers of yoga

12.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते । / श्रद्धया परयोपेताः ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥
LIT: Those who, having fixed the mind on me, worship me, always yoked, endowed with supreme faith: they are held by me to be the most yoked.
WORDS: mayi = on me; āveśya = having fixed; manaḥ = the mind; ye = those who; mām = me; nitya-yuktāḥ = always yoked; upāsate = they worship; śraddhayā = with faith; parayā = with supreme; upetāḥ = endowed; te = they; me = by me; yukta-tamāḥ = the most yoked; matāḥ = are held to be

12.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते । / सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यञ्च कूटस्थमचलन्ध्रुवम् ॥
LIT: But those who worship the imperishable, the indefinable, the unmanifest, which goes everywhere and is unthinkable, the unchanging, the unmoving, the fixed,
WORDS: ye = those who; tu = but; akṣaram = the imperishable; anirdeśyam = the indefinable; avyaktam = the unmanifest; paryupāsate = they worship; sarvatra-gam = going everywhere; acintyam = the unthinkable; ca = and; kūṭa-stham = the unchanging; acalam = the unmoving; dhruvam = the fixed
NOTE: Verses 3 to 4 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.
NOTE: "The unchanging" can also be read as "that which stands at the summit".

12.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सन्नियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः । / ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥
LIT: having restrained the whole group of the senses, of even buddhi everywhere, they reach me alone, delighting in the welfare of all beings.
WORDS: sanniyamya = having restrained; indriya-grāmam = the whole group of the senses; sarvatra = everywhere; sama-buddhayaḥ = of even buddhi; te = they; prāpnuvanti = they reach; mām = me; eva = alone; sarva-bhūta-hite = in the welfare of all beings; ratāḥ = delighting
NOTE: "They reach me alone" can also be read as "they too reach me".

12.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् । / अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥
LIT: The trouble is greater for those whose minds are attached to the unmanifest, for the unmanifest goal is reached with pain by those who have bodies.
WORDS: kleśaḥ = the trouble; adhika-taraḥ = greater; teṣām = of those; avyakta-āsakta-cetasām = whose minds are attached to the unmanifest; avyaktā = unmanifest; hi = for; gatiḥ = the goal; duḥkham = with pain; dehavadbhiḥ = by those who have bodies; avāpyate = is reached

12.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः । / अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥
LIT: But those who, having laid down all actions in me, holding me as the highest, worship me, meditating on me by a yoga that indeed has no other object,
WORDS: ye = those who; tu = but; sarvāṇi = all; karmāṇi = actions; mayi = in me; saṁnyasya = having laid down; mat-parāḥ = holding me as the highest; ananyena = with no other; eva = indeed; yogena = by yoga; mām = me; dhyāyantaḥ = meditating on; upāsate = they worship
NOTE: Verses 6 to 7 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

12.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् । / भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥
LIT: of them, whose minds are fixed on me, I become before long the one who lifts them out from the ocean of death and wandering on, O son of Pṛthā.
WORDS: teṣām = of them; aham = I; samuddhartā = the one who lifts out; mṛtyu-saṁsāra-sāgarāt = from the ocean of death and wandering on; bhavāmi = I become; nacirāt = before long; pārtha = O son of Pṛthā; mayi = on me; āveśita-cetasām = whose minds are fixed

12.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय । / निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥
LIT: Place the mind on me alone, make buddhi enter into me. From this onward you will dwell in me alone. There is no doubt.
WORDS: mayi = on me; eva = alone; manaḥ = the mind; ādhatsva = place; mayi = into me; buddhim = buddhi; niveśaya = make enter; nivasiṣyasi = you will dwell; mayi = in me; eva = alone; ataḥ = from this; ūrdhvam = onward; na = not; saṁśayaḥ = doubt

12.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् । / अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय ॥
LIT: Now if you are not able to settle the mind steadily on me, then seek to reach me by the yoga of practice, O winner of wealth.
WORDS: atha = now if; cittam = the mind; samādhātum = to settle; na = not; śaknoṣi = you are able; mayi = on me; sthiram = steadily; abhyāsa-yogena = by the yoga of practice; tataḥ = then; mām = me; iccha = seek; āptum = to reach; dhanañjaya = O winner of wealth

12.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव । / मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥
LIT: If you are unable even in practice, become one for whom action for me is the highest. Even doing actions for my sake, you will reach perfection.
WORDS: abhyāse = in practice; api = even; asamarthaḥ = unable; asi = you are; mat-karma-paramaḥ = one for whom action for me is the highest; bhava = become; mat-artham = for my sake; api = even; karmāṇi = actions; kurvan = doing; siddhim = perfection; avāpsyasi = you will reach

12.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः । / सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥
LIT: Now if you are unable to do even this, then, resorting to my yoga, with the self restrained, give up the fruit of all action.
WORDS: atha = now if; etat = this; api = even; aśaktaḥ = unable; asi = you are; kartum = to do; mat-yogam = to my yoga; āśritaḥ = resorting; sarva-karma-phala-tyāgam = the giving up of the fruit of all action; tataḥ = then; kuru = do; yata-ātmavān = with the self restrained
NOTE: "Then, resorting to my yoga" can also be read with the first half: "if you are unable to do even this while resorting to my yoga".

12.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते । / ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥
LIT: For knowledge is better than practice, meditation surpasses knowledge, and the giving up of the fruit of action surpasses meditation. From giving up, peace comes immediately after.
WORDS: śreyaḥ = better; hi = for; jñānam = knowledge; abhyāsāt = than practice; jñānāt = than knowledge; dhyānam = meditation; viśiṣyate = it surpasses; dhyānāt = than meditation; karma-phala-tyāgaḥ = the giving up of the fruit of action; tyāgāt = from giving up; śāntiḥ = peace; anantaram = immediately after

12.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च । / निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥
LIT: One who does not hate any of all beings, friendly and indeed compassionate, without the sense of mine, without the sense of I, the same in pain and pleasure, patient,
WORDS: adveṣṭā = one who does not hate; sarva-bhūtānām = of all beings; maitraḥ = friendly; karuṇaḥ = compassionate; eva = indeed; ca = and; nirmamaḥ = without the sense of mine; nirahaṅkāraḥ = without the sense of I; sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ = the same in pain and pleasure; kṣamī = patient
NOTE: Verses 13 to 14 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

12.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः । / मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥
LIT: always content, a yogī, with the self restrained, of firm resolve, with mind and buddhi offered to me: he who is devoted to me is dear to me.
WORDS: santuṣṭaḥ = content; satatam = always; yogī = a yogī; yata-ātmā = with the self restrained; dṛḍha-niścayaḥ = of firm resolve; mayi = to me; arpita-manaḥ-buddhiḥ = with mind and buddhi offered; yaḥ = he who; mat-bhaktaḥ = devoted to me; saḥ = he; me = to me; priyaḥ = dear
NOTE: "Always content, a yogī" can also be read as "content, always a yogī".

12.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः । / हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥
LIT: He from whom the world does not shrink, and who does not shrink from the world, who is freed from joy, impatience, fear and agitation: he too is dear to me.
WORDS: yasmāt = from whom; na = not; udvijate = it shrinks; lokaḥ = the world; lokāt = from the world; na = not; udvijate = he shrinks; ca = and; yaḥ = who; harṣa-amarṣa-bhaya-udvegaiḥ = from joy, impatience, fear and agitation; muktaḥ = freed; yaḥ = who; saḥ = he; ca = too; me = to me; priyaḥ = dear

12.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः । / सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥
LIT: Expecting nothing, pure, skilled, sitting apart, with distress gone, giving up all undertakings: he who is devoted to me is dear to me.
WORDS: anapekṣaḥ = expecting nothing; śuciḥ = pure; dakṣaḥ = skilled; udāsīnaḥ = sitting apart; gata-vyathaḥ = with distress gone; sarva-ārambha-parityāgī = giving up all undertakings; yaḥ = he who; mat-bhaktaḥ = devoted to me; saḥ = he; me = to me; priyaḥ = dear

12.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति । / शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥
LIT: He who does not rejoice, does not hate, does not grieve, does not long, giving up the auspicious and the inauspicious, who is full of devotion: he is dear to me.
WORDS: yaḥ = he who; na = not; hṛṣyati = he rejoices; na = not; dveṣṭi = he hates; na = not; śocati = he grieves; na = not; kāṅkṣati = he longs; śubha-aśubha-parityāgī = giving up the auspicious and the inauspicious; bhaktimān = full of devotion; yaḥ = who; saḥ = he; me = to me; priyaḥ = dear

12.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः । / शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥
LIT: The same toward an enemy and toward a friend, and likewise in honour and dishonour, the same in cold and heat, pleasure and pain, free from attachment,
WORDS: samaḥ = the same; śatrau = toward an enemy; ca = and; mitre = toward a friend; ca = and; tathā = likewise; māna-apamānayoḥ = in honour and dishonour; śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkheṣu = in cold and heat, pleasure and pain; samaḥ = the same; saṅga-vivarjitaḥ = free from attachment
NOTE: Verses 18 to 19 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

12.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् । / अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥
LIT: to whom blame and praise are equal, silent, content with anything whatever, without a home, of steady mind, full of devotion: that man is dear to me.
WORDS: tulya-nindā-stutiḥ = to whom blame and praise are equal; maunī = silent; santuṣṭaḥ = content; yena = with whatever; kenacit = with anything; aniketaḥ = without a home; sthira-matiḥ = of steady mind; bhaktimān = full of devotion; me = to me; priyaḥ = dear; naraḥ = the man

12.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते । / श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥
LIT: But those who honour this nectar that accords with dharma, as spoken, having faith, holding me as the highest, devotees: they are exceedingly dear to me.
WORDS: ye = those who; tu = but; dharmya-amṛtam = the nectar that accords with dharma; idam = this; yathā-uktam = as spoken; paryupāsate = they honour; śraddadhānāḥ = having faith; mat-paramāḥ = holding me as the highest; bhaktāḥ = devotees; te = they; atīva = exceedingly; me = to me; priyāḥ = dear
NOTE: "This nectar that accords with dharma" can also be read as "this immortal dharma".
