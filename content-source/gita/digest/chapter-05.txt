5.1 [Arjuna speaks]
SA: संन्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंससि । / यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रूहि सुनिश्चितम् ॥
LIT: You praise the renunciation of actions, O Kṛṣṇa, and again yoga. Which one of these two is better, tell me that, well determined.
WORDS: saṁnyāsam = the renunciation; karmaṇām = of actions; kṛṣṇa = O Kṛṣṇa; punaḥ = again; yogam = yoga; ca = and; śaṁsasi = you praise; yat = which; śreyaḥ = better; etayoḥ = of these two; ekam = one; tat = that; me = to me; brūhi = tell; su-niścitam = well determined

5.2 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: संन्यासः कर्मयोगश्च निःश्रेयसकरावुभौ । / तयोस्तु कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो विशिष्यते ॥
LIT: Renunciation and the yoga of action both bring about the highest good. But of the two, the yoga of action excels the renunciation of action.
WORDS: saṁnyāsaḥ = renunciation; karma-yogaḥ = the yoga of action; ca = and; niḥśreyasa-karau = bringing about the highest good; ubhau = both; tayoḥ = of the two; tu = but; karma-saṁnyāsāt = than the renunciation of action; karma-yogaḥ = the yoga of action; viśiṣyate = excels

5.3 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ज्ञेयः स नित्यसंन्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न काङ्क्षति । / निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते ॥
LIT: He is to be known as a constant renouncer who neither hates nor longs. For one free from the pairs of opposites, O mighty-armed one, is easily released from bondage.
WORDS: jñeyaḥ = to be known; saḥ = he; nitya-saṁnyāsī = a constant renouncer; yaḥ = who; na = not; dveṣṭi = hates; na = not; kāṅkṣati = longs; nirdvandvaḥ = free from the pairs of opposites; hi = for; mahā-bāho = O mighty-armed one; sukham = easily; bandhāt = from bondage; pramucyate = is released

5.4 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: साङ्ख्ययोगौ पृथग्बालाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः । / एकमप्यास्थितः सम्यगुभयोर्विन्दते फलम् ॥
LIT: The childish, not the learned, declare sāṅkhya and yoga to be separate. One who is rightly established in even one finds the fruit of both.
WORDS: sāṅkhya-yogau = sāṅkhya and yoga; pṛthak = separate; bālāḥ = the childish; pravadanti = declare; na = not; paṇḍitāḥ = the learned; ekam = in one; api = even; āsthitaḥ = established; samyak = rightly; ubhayoḥ = of both; vindate = finds; phalam = the fruit

5.5 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यत्साङ्ख्यैः प्राप्यते स्थानं तद्योगैरपि गम्यते । / एकं साङ्ख्यं च योगं च यः पश्यति स पश्यति ॥
LIT: The place that is reached by the followers of sāṅkhya, that is also gone to by the followers of yoga. One who sees sāṅkhya and yoga as one, he sees.
WORDS: yat = which; sāṅkhyaiḥ = by the followers of sāṅkhya; prāpyate = is reached; sthānam = the place; tat = that; yogaiḥ = by the followers of yoga; api = also; gamyate = is gone to; ekam = one; sāṅkhyam = sāṅkhya; ca = and; yogam = yoga; ca = and; yaḥ = who; paśyati = sees; saḥ = he; paśyati = sees

5.6 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: संन्यासस्तु महाबाहो दुःखमाप्तुमयोगतः । / योगयुक्तो मुनिर्ब्रह्म नचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥
LIT: But renunciation, O mighty-armed one, is hard to reach without yoga. The sage yoked by yoga goes to brahman before long.
WORDS: saṁnyāsaḥ = renunciation; tu = but; mahā-bāho = O mighty-armed one; duḥkham = hard; āptum = to reach; ayogataḥ = without yoga; yoga-yuktaḥ = yoked by yoga; muniḥ = the sage; brahma = to brahman; nacireṇa = before long; adhigacchati = goes

5.7 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः । / सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते ॥
LIT: One who is yoked by yoga, whose self is purified, whose self is conquered, whose senses are conquered, whose self has become the self of all beings, is not smeared even while acting.
WORDS: yoga-yuktaḥ = yoked by yoga; viśuddha-ātmā = whose self is purified; vijita-ātmā = whose self is conquered; jita-indriyaḥ = whose senses are conquered; sarva-bhūta-ātma-bhūta-ātmā = whose self has become the self of all beings; kurvan = acting; api = even; na = not; lipyate = is smeared

5.8 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नैव किञ्चित्करोमीति युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित् । / पश्यञ्शृण्वन्स्पृशञ्जिघ्रन्नश्नन्गच्छन्स्वपञ्श्वसन् ॥
LIT: "I do not do anything at all": thus the yoked one, the knower of the truth, should think, while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, going, sleeping, breathing,
WORDS: na = not; eva = at all; kiñcit = anything; karomi = I do; iti = thus; yuktaḥ = the yoked one; manyeta = should think; tattva-vit = the knower of the truth; paśyan = seeing; śṛṇvan = hearing; spṛśan = touching; jighran = smelling; aśnan = eating; gacchan = going; svapan = sleeping; śvasan = breathing
NOTE: Verses 8 to 9 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

5.9 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: प्रलपन्विसृजन्गृह्णन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि । / इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु वर्तन्त इति धारयन् ॥
LIT: talking, letting go, grasping, even opening the eyes and closing the eyes, holding thus: "The senses move among the objects of the senses."
WORDS: pralapan = talking; visṛjan = letting go; gṛhṇan = grasping; unmiṣan = opening the eyes; nimiṣan = closing the eyes; api = even; indriyāṇi = the senses; indriya-artheṣu = among the objects of the senses; vartante = move; iti = thus; dhārayan = holding
NOTE: "Letting go" can also be read as "excreting".

5.10 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः । / लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा ॥
LIT: One who acts having placed actions in brahman, having given up attachment, he is not smeared by evil, like a lotus leaf by water.
WORDS: brahmaṇi = in brahman; ādhāya = having placed; karmāṇi = actions; saṅgam = attachment; tyaktvā = having given up; karoti = acts; yaḥ = who; lipyate = is smeared; na = not; saḥ = he; pāpena = by evil; padma-patram = a lotus leaf; iva = like; ambhasā = by water

5.11 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि । / योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वात्मशुद्धये ॥
LIT: With the body, with the mind, with the buddhi, even with the senses alone, yogīs do action, having given up attachment, for the purification of the self.
WORDS: kāyena = with the body; manasā = with the mind; buddhyā = with the buddhi; kevalaiḥ = alone; indriyaiḥ = with the senses; api = even; yoginaḥ = yogīs; karma = action; kurvanti = do; saṅgam = attachment; tyaktvā = having given up; ātma-śuddhaye = for the purification of the self

5.12 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: युक्तः कर्मफलं त्यक्त्वा शान्तिमाप्नोति नैष्ठिकीम् । / अयुक्तः कामकारेण फले सक्तो निबध्यते ॥
LIT: The yoked one, having given up the fruit of action, attains final peace. The unyoked one, attached to the fruit by the working of desire, is bound.
WORDS: yuktaḥ = the yoked one; karma-phalam = the fruit of action; tyaktvā = having given up; śāntim = peace; āpnoti = attains; naiṣṭhikīm = final; ayuktaḥ = the unyoked one; kāma-kāreṇa = by the working of desire; phale = to the fruit; saktaḥ = attached; nibadhyate = is bound
NOTE: "Final peace" can also be read as "the peace that comes from steadfastness".

5.13 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: सर्वकर्माणि मनसा संन्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी । / नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन् ॥
LIT: Having renounced all actions with the mind, the embodied one sits happily, in control, in the city of nine gates, not acting at all and not causing action.
WORDS: sarva-karmāṇi = all actions; manasā = with the mind; saṁnyasya = having renounced; āste = sits; sukham = happily; vaśī = in control; nava-dvāre = of nine gates; pure = in the city; dehī = the embodied one; na = not; eva = at all; kurvan = acting; na = not; kārayan = causing action

5.14 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न कर्तृत्वं न कर्माणि लोकस्य सृजति प्रभुः । / न कर्मफलसंयोगं स्वभावस्तु प्रवर्तते ॥
LIT: The lord does not create the doership of the world, nor its actions, nor the joining of action with its fruit. But one's own nature goes forward.
WORDS: na = not; kartṛtvam = doership; na = not; karmāṇi = actions; lokasya = of the world; sṛjati = creates; prabhuḥ = the lord; na = not; karma-phala-saṁyogam = the joining of action with its fruit; svabhāvaḥ = one's own nature; tu = but; pravartate = goes forward
NOTE: "The lord" can be read as the self, the master of the body, or as the Lord of the universe.

5.15 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः । / अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः ॥
LIT: The all-pervading one does not take on the evil of anyone, and indeed not the good deed. Knowledge is covered by ignorance. By that, creatures are deluded.
WORDS: na = not; ādatte = takes on; kasyacit = of anyone; pāpam = the evil; na = not; ca = and; eva = indeed; sukṛtam = the good deed; vibhuḥ = the all-pervading one; ajñānena = by ignorance; āvṛtam = covered; jñānam = knowledge; tena = by that; muhyanti = are deluded; jantavaḥ = creatures

5.16 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ज्ञानेन तु तदज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः । / तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम् ॥
LIT: But for those whose ignorance is destroyed by knowledge of the self, their knowledge, like the sun, lights up that supreme.
WORDS: jñānena = by knowledge; tu = but; tat = that; ajñānam = ignorance; yeṣām = of whom; nāśitam = destroyed; ātmanaḥ = of the self; teṣām = of them; āditya-vat = like the sun; jñānam = knowledge; prakāśayati = lights up; tat = that; param = supreme
NOTE: "Whose ignorance is destroyed by knowledge of the self" can also be read as "whose ignorance of the self is destroyed by knowledge".

5.17 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः । / गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः ॥
LIT: Those whose buddhi is in that, whose self is that, whose foundation is that, whose highest goal is that, whose stains are shaken off by knowledge, go to no return.
WORDS: tat-buddhayaḥ = whose buddhi is in that; tat-ātmānaḥ = whose self is that; tat-niṣṭhāḥ = whose foundation is that; tat-parāyaṇāḥ = whose highest goal is that; gacchanti = they go; apunar-āvṛttim = to no return; jñāna-nirdhūta-kalmaṣāḥ = whose stains are shaken off by knowledge

5.18 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: विद्याविनयसम्पन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि । / शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः ॥
LIT: In a brāhmaṇa endowed with learning and humility, in a cow, in an elephant, and even in a dog and in one who cooks dogs, the learned see the same.
WORDS: vidyā-vinaya-sampanne = endowed with learning and humility; brāhmaṇe = in a brāhmaṇa; gavi = in a cow; hastini = in an elephant; śuni = in a dog; ca = and; eva = even; śva-pāke = in one who cooks dogs; ca = and; paṇḍitāḥ = the learned; sama-darśinaḥ = seeing the same

5.19 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः । / निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद् ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः ॥
LIT: Right here creation is conquered by those whose mind stands in sameness. For brahman is flawless and the same. Therefore they stand in brahman.
WORDS: iha = here; eva = right; taiḥ = by them; jitaḥ = conquered; sargaḥ = creation; yeṣām = of whom; sāmye = in sameness; sthitam = standing; manaḥ = the mind; nirdoṣam = flawless; hi = for; samam = the same; brahma = brahman; tasmāt = therefore; brahmaṇi = in brahman; te = they; sthitāḥ = standing
NOTE: "Creation" can also be read as "birth", meaning the round of being born again.

5.20 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् । / स्थिरबुद्धिरसम्मूढो ब्रह्मविद् ब्रह्मणि स्थितः ॥
LIT: One should not rejoice having got the pleasant, and one should not shudder having got the unpleasant. The knower of brahman, whose buddhi is steady, undeluded, stands in brahman.
WORDS: na = not; prahṛṣyet = one should rejoice; priyam = the pleasant; prāpya = having got; na = not; udvijet = one should shudder; prāpya = having got; ca = and; apriyam = the unpleasant; sthira-buddhiḥ = whose buddhi is steady; asammūḍhaḥ = undeluded; brahma-vit = the knower of brahman; brahmaṇi = in brahman; sthitaḥ = standing

5.21 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम् । / स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षयमश्नुते ॥
LIT: One whose self is unattached to outer contacts finds the happiness which is in the self. He, whose self is yoked by the yoga of brahman, attains undecaying happiness.
WORDS: bāhya-sparśeṣu = to outer contacts; asakta-ātmā = whose self is unattached; vindati = finds; ātmani = in the self; yat = which; sukham = happiness; saḥ = he; brahma-yoga-yukta-ātmā = whose self is yoked by the yoga of brahman; sukham = happiness; akṣayam = undecaying; aśnute = attains
NOTE: "Whose self is yoked by the yoga of brahman" can also be read as "whose self is yoked to brahman by yoga".

5.22 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते । / आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः ॥
LIT: For the enjoyments which are born of contact, they are only wombs of pain. They have a beginning and an end, O son of Kuntī. The wise one does not delight in them.
WORDS: ye = which; hi = for; saṁsparśa-jāḥ = born of contact; bhogāḥ = enjoyments; duḥkha-yonayaḥ = wombs of pain; eva = only; te = they; ādi-anta-vantaḥ = having a beginning and an end; kaunteya = O son of Kuntī; na = not; teṣu = in them; ramate = delights; budhaḥ = the wise one

5.23 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्शरीरविमोक्षणात् । / कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः ॥
LIT: One who is able right here, before release from the body, to bear the rush arising from desire and anger, he is yoked, he is a happy man.
WORDS: śaknoti = is able; iha = here; eva = right; yaḥ = who; soḍhum = to bear; prāk = before; śarīra-vimokṣaṇāt = release from the body; kāma-krodha-udbhavam = arising from desire and anger; vegam = the rush; saḥ = he; yuktaḥ = yoked; saḥ = he; sukhī = happy; naraḥ = a man

5.24 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: योऽन्तःसुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव यः । / स योगी ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति ॥

WORDS: yaḥ = who; antaḥ-sukhaḥ = whose happiness is within; antar-ārāmaḥ = whose delight is within; tathā = likewise; antar-jyotiḥ = whose light is within; eva = only; yaḥ = who; saḥ = that; yogī = yogī; brahma-nirvāṇam = to the nirvāṇa of brahman; brahma-bhūtaḥ = having become brahman; adhigacchati = goes
NOTE: "The nirvāṇa of brahman" can also be read as "nirvāṇa in brahman", here and in the next two verses.

5.25 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः । / छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥

5.26 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाम् । / अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम् ॥

NOTE: "On every side" can also be read as "close at hand".

5.27 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे भ्रुवोः । / प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ ॥

NOTE: Verses 27 to 28 are one sentence in the Sanskrit.

5.28 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः । / विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः ॥

WORDS: yata-indriya-manaḥ-buddhiḥ = whose senses, mind and buddhi are restrained; muniḥ = the sage; mokṣa-parāyaṇaḥ = whose highest goal is release; vigata-icchā-bhaya-krodhaḥ = from whom wish, fear and anger have gone; yaḥ = who; sadā = always; muktaḥ = freed; eva = indeed; saḥ = he
NOTE: "He who is always so is freed indeed" can also be read as "he is always freed indeed".

5.29 [Kṛṣṇa speaks]
SA: भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम् । / सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति ॥
LIT: Having known me as the enjoyer of sacrifices and austerities, the great lord of all the worlds, the friend of all beings, one goes to peace.
WORDS: bhoktāram = the enjoyer; yajña-tapasām = of sacrifices and austerities; sarva-loka-mahā-īśvaram = the great lord of all the worlds; suhṛdam = the friend; sarva-bhūtānām = of all beings; jñātvā = having known; mām = me; śāntim = to peace; ṛcchati = one goes
